## Executive Summary

**FO3 compatibility remains HIGH, and the one HIGH regression on record at the 2026-09-19 baseline
is now confirmed fixed.** This cycle found **0 new or regressed FO3-specific findings** across all
5 dimensions. Every real-data regression guard re-run this session — the NIF/particle real-data lane,
the ESM parse-rate probe, the Megaton interior REFR count, the object-LOD scheme table, the B-spline
animation NaN-safety suite, the FO3 MenuXml HUD corpus, and the Stimpak/Hardcore consumable guards —
passed against the live GOTY install with figures identical to the baseline or improved by an interim
fix.

**The baseline's HIGH (FO3-D2-2026-09-19-01 / #4467)** — "#4261's per-system controller scoping
drops most authored emitter birth rates, FO3 coverage 94.3% → 70.8%" — is **fixed**. `ee3e3a4c8`

(`real_archive_torch_meshes_surface_particle_emitters`, `--ignored`): FO3 now measures
**143/149 = 96.0%**, above both the red 70.8% and the pre-regression 94.3% on record. #3754's
per-game floor gate is green for all 4 archives swept.

**Two other baseline items re-verified genuinely fixed, not merely re-parsed-and-dropped**:
- **FO3-D2-2026-09-19-02 / #4398** (re-verified-open MEDIUM at baseline: emitter spawn cone stays
  world-axis-aligned) — now fixed end-to-end. `local_rotation` in
  `crates/nif/src/import/walk/emitter.rs:796-820` carries the composed host-chain × block rotation,
  and it is consumed downstream at `byroredux/src/scene/nif_loader.rs` and
  `byroredux/src/cell_loader/spawn.rs`, not just parsed and left unread.
- **#4401** (baseline measured 0 residual instances before the fix; carries the `game:fo3` label) —
  closed at the source.

**Delta vs FNV**: unchanged. FO3 and FNV still share the NIF block parser, both inline-shader
properties, the ESM record schemas (FO3 remains a strict subset of FNV's `NPC_`/`DIAL`/`INFO`
subforms), `CELL.XCLL`, the WRLD/CLMT/WTHR chain, the `FalloutLegacyBlocks` object-LOD scheme and the
BSA v104 reader. A fix in any of these benefits both games; a regression (as #4467 was) hits both.

**One shared-mechanism MEDIUM reproduces on FO3 data and is cited, not re-filed**: `LTEX.GNAM`
(`ESM-2026-09-21-D2-03`, filed and **OPEN as #4642**) decodes a grass array into a one-grass-per-LTEX
map (last wins). Independently re-measured against `Fallout3.esm`: 9 grass-bearing LTEX records, 2

**Two shared-mechanism findings from yesterday's `/audit-exterior` leg also reach FO3, not yet filed
as GitHub issues, cited here**: a `WindField` frame/sign mismatch (MEDIUM, SpeedTree billboards lean
opposite the grass on FO3 east-west wind) and a dead-editor-default creek current direction (LOW, 2/2
FO3 River-by-name records affected).

**Two new HIGH findings from today's `/audit-scripting` leg reach FO3's newly-executing quest
scripts** (M47.3's `obscript_vm.rs`, a capability this skill's own 2026-09-19 baseline flagged as the

scripting call sites. Neither is filed yet; both are owned by `/audit-scripting` and cited here for
their FO3 reach.
