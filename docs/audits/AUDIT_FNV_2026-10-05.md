### Dimension 2 — ESM Data Slice (FalloutNV.esm)

#### FNV-2026-10-05-D2-01: #5223's dismember-trigger recognizer only matches a trigger and its target loaded in the same `load_references_budgeted` call. An exterior trigger whose `XLKR` target is a persistent-CELL ref is never recognised, which leaves 29 FO3 live-base corpses alive, and the census guard cannot see it

- **Severity**: MEDIUM. This is the same impact class as #5223, which was filed MEDIUM.
  - FNV impact is nil today, because the 4 cross-cell FNV targets are base-health corpses that
    #5005 already covers.
  - FO3 impact is 29 actors that #5223 meant to kill.
- **Dimension**: ESM Data Slice (authored corpses). The mechanism belongs to `/audit-gameplay`.
  FO3 re-verifies under `/audit-fo3`.
- **Location**:
  - `byroredux/src/cell_loader/reference_state.rs:396-437`: `script_killed_corpse_forms`
    builds Pass 1's `killed` set from triggers in `refs` only.
  - `byroredux/src/cell_loader/references/mod.rs:335-336`: the per-call invocation.
  - `byroredux/src/cell_loader/references/mod.rs:763`: the consumer.
  - `byroredux/src/cell_loader/exterior.rs:293-294`: the persistent CELL is loaded with its
    `local_refs`.
  - `byroredux/src/cell_loader/exterior.rs:2162-2163`: each exterior cell is loaded with its
    own `cell.references`.
  - `byroredux/src/cell_loader/reference_state.rs:662-730`: the census test.
- **Status**: NEW. #5223 is closed, and this is an incomplete fix of it, not a regression.
  - SCR-D6-2026-10-05-01 covers EDID-based matching, which is a different defect.
  - No sibling report or issue mentions persistent, cross-cell or locality.
- **Description**:
  - `load_references_budgeted` calls `script_killed_corpse_forms(refs, record_index)`. Pass 1
    marks the `XLKR` targets of dismember-family triggers *found in `refs`*. The consumer then
    stamps `apply_starts_dead` only on a placement in the same `refs`.
  - Exterior placements come in two calls:
    - the worldspace persistent CELL (`PersistentCellApplyJob::advance`, `local_refs`), which
      holds persistent actors;
    - one call per temporary exterior cell, which holds the non-persistent trigger activators.
  - A trigger and a persistent target therefore never share a call. When the trigger's cell
    loads, its target was spawned earlier by the persistent apply, and the set the persistent
    apply computed held no triggers.
  - The census test unions `script_killed_corpse_forms` over every cell and counts link
    targets globally. It passes whether or not the target sits in the trigger's cell.
- **Evidence**: scratch probe over the shipped masters. It resolves each dismember-trigger
  target to the cell list that places it and prints the target's base.
  ```
  FNV (10 targets; 6 same-cell interior, 4 cross-cell):
  TRIGGER 000CEDC9 in [EXT wastelandnv (7, -9)] -> 000CEDCA in PERSIST wastelandnv  base DEADRaider1MeleeCF hp=0 sd=true
  TRIGGER 000CD726 in [EXT wastelandnv (3, -8)] -> 000CD346 in PERSIST wastelandnv  base CrFeralGhoulGlowingOneDEAD sd=true
  TRIGGER 000CEE23 in [EXT wastelandnv (8, -9)] -> 000CEE22 in PERSIST wastelandnv  base DEADWastelanderAAMNV hp=0 sd=true
  TRIGGER 0014E835 in [EXT freesidenorthworld (1,-2)] -> 0014E836 in PERSIST freesidenorthworld base VMS49RangefinderCourer hp=0 sd=true
  (live-base FNV targets are all same-cell interiors: LvlFiendGunVault3 hp50 / vault3b,
   NVProspectorMale hp60 / SLscorpionBurrowINT, NVCrGoldenGecko / vault34a — recognised)
  FO3: 85 targets; 54 in a PERSIST cell with the trigger in a temp EXT cell; 29 of those not sd:
    10 LvlSupermutantGunDISMEMBERNOAMMO, 6 LvlSupermutantMeleeDISMEMBERNOAMMO,
    5 LvlWastelanderDISMEMBER, 4 MS06SuperMutantGunDEAD, 2 LvlSupermutantGunDISMEMBER,
    1 MS06SuperMutantMeleeDEAD, 1 DeadSuperMutant2Melee
  ```
  - The repo's own `corpse_trigger_probe` example prints a target only when it is in the same
    cell. On FNV it prints 4 `TARGET:` lines (it filters creatures), not the census's 10.
  - `fo3_fnv_script_killed_corpses_match_the_measured_census` passes (10 + 2 on FNV).
- **Impact**:
  - FO3: 29 of the 49 positive-health corpses #5223 was filed for still spawn as live, armed,
    hostile actors (#4414) in Capital Wasteland exteriors, and cannot be looted.
  - FNV: no current impact. Any FNV DLC or mod that links a dismember trigger to a persistent
    live-base actor would hit the same gap.
  - The guard reports the fix as complete.
- **Related**: #5223, #5005, SCR-D6-2026-10-05-01, the AUDIT_SAVE_2026-10-05 note on #5223 and
  v32, GAME-D4-2026-10-05-01.
- **Suggested Fix**:
  - Compute the set once per load order, over every cell in the index (FormIDs are global), and
    store it as a resource. The streaming and persistent paths then consult the same set.
  - This also removes the per-resume recomputation, which today re-runs the scan and clones
    editor-id strings across FNV's 4,495 `XLKR` placements on every budgeted resume.
  - Make the census test assert locality-independent recognition. Feed it each cell's refs
    separately and require the union of per-call results to equal 85 + 3 (FO3) and 10 + 2
    (FNV).
