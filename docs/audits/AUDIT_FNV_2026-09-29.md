### Dimension 2 — ESM Data Slice (FalloutNV.esm)

#### FNV-2026-09-29-D2-01: FNV's authored corpses are marked by `XRGD` ragdoll data, not a header flag, and #4814's Starts Dead decode never fires on FO3/FNV, so every placed corpse spawns alive

- **Severity**: HIGH. This is the same impact class as #4814, which was HIGH: corpses stand up, cannot be looted, and hostile ones attack.
- **Dimension**: ESM Data Slice. The decode belongs to `/audit-esm` and consumption to `/audit-gameplay`. The FNV data half is owned here.
- **Location**:
  - `crates/plugin/src/esm/cell/walkers.rs:1190-1195`: `starts_dead = ACHR && Tes5Plus && flags & 0x200`.
  - `crates/plugin/src/esm/reader.rs:108-114`: `FLAG_STARTS_DEAD`, cited from xEdit's TES5 list.
  - `crates/plugin/src/esm/cell/mod.rs:569-574`: the field doc says "FO3/FNV ship none".
  - `byroredux/src/cell_loader/references/mod.rs:747-754`: the only consumer, `apply_starts_dead`.
- **Status**: NEW.
  - #4814 (CLOSED) fixed the Skyrim/FO4 bit only.
  - AUDIT_ESM_2026-09-29 (line 258) notes that the `0x200` gate is inert on FO3/FNV. It does not mention that FNV authors corpses another way.
  - `gh` searches for "XRGD", "corpse" and "starts dead" found no matching issue.
- **Description**:
  - xEdit's FNV definitions (`wbDefinitionsFNV.pas`, dev-4.1.6) give ACHR the record flags {10 Persistent, 11 Initially Disabled, 25 No AI Acquire}. ACRE adds 15 Visible When Distant.
  - Neither record type has a Starts Dead bit. Both carry `wbRagdoll` (`XRGD` / `XRGB`).
  - FNV's placed corpses therefore carry no header signal. What they carry is an authored ragdoll pose.
  - The engine decodes no `XRGD`: `rg XRGD` over `crates/` and `byroredux/` returns 0 code hits. `starts_dead` is therefore false for every FO3/FNV actor.
  - These actors go through the normal actor job, get their base's `PKID` packages and ambient AI, and are armed by #4414 faction hostility.
- **Evidence**: a byte scan of FalloutNV.esm (`/tmp/audit/fnv/scan/flags.py`, `xrgd.py`; group headers are 24 bytes, compressed bodies decoded as raw deflate):
  - Header flag `0x200`: 0 of 3,386 ACHRs and 0 of 2,999 ACREs.
  - 387 actor refs carry `XRGD`: 278 ACHRs and 109 ACREs.
  - **343 of the 387** place a base whose EditorID contains `dead` or `corpse`. Examples: `VHDDeadNCRTrooper`, `VHDDeadLegionary`, `VHDDeadCenturion`, `NVProspectorMaleDEADLite`, `NVProspectorMaleDEADSulfurCave03`.
  - Most of the remaining 44 are deactivated robots (`SSHQProtectronBroken`, `SSHQMrHandyBroken`).
  - Only 14 non-`XRGD` refs place a Dead-named base.
  - Header flags on the 387: `0x0` ×209, `0x400` ×146, `0xC00` ×19, `0x800` ×10. So about 358 are not initially disabled and spawn at cell load.
  - 29 of them use bases scripted with `VHDDeadSafetySCRIPT` (`Begin OnLoad / If (GetDead == 0) / Kill`). This is a safety net for an actor that is normally dead already. The engine has no object-script `Kill` either.
  - Fallout3.esm has the same pattern: 498 `XRGD` actor refs, 268 on Dead- or Corpse-named bases and 230 on `Loot1*` corpse bases. FO3 is re-verified by `/audit-fo3`.
- **Impact**:
  - Hundreds of FNV corpses become live NPCs running their packages. They include the Hoover Dam battlefield dead (Legion corpses are hostile), the Prospector corpses in the caves, and the broken Securitron HQ robots.
  - Corpses cannot be looted: loot is gated on `Dead`.
  - A hostile corpse starts combat on sight (#4414).
  - The #4814 field doc tells the next reader that FO3/FNV have no corpses, and that is false.
- **Related**: #4814, #4817, #4693 (corpse root), #4772 (FO3 restored-corpse ragdoll), AUDIT_ESM_2026-09-29 line 258, GAME-D4-2026-09-24-01.
- **Suggested Fix**:
  - First confirm the engine rule. `XRGD` presence on an ACHR/ACRE is the only FO3/FNV corpse signal in the data, but no xEdit or GECK text documents it as "starts dead". Per the no-guessing policy, cite a source before shipping.
  - Then decode `XRGD` presence on ACHR/ACRE for the 24-byte FO3/FNV variant into `PlacedRef::starts_dead`, or into a sibling field. Keep the `0x200` path for Skyrim and later.
  - Correct the field doc.
  - Add a real-master census guard (`FalloutNV.esm` `XRGD`-actor count ≥ 380).
  - Later, consume the `XRGD` pose as the corpse's initial ragdoll pose.
