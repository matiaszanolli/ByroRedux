### FNV-SD-01: Model-less templated `CREA` shells spawn nothing - 1,877 FNV creature placements (63%) never get a skeleton, mesh, AI or identity
- **Severity**: HIGH
- **Dimension**: 4/5 (creature spawn at the NPC-spawn boundary)
- **Location**: `byroredux/src/npc_spawn/resumable/runtime.rs:445-480` (`prepare_creature_state` derives the skeleton from the **shell's own** `npc.model_path`), `:543-546` (`if state.skeleton_path.is_empty() { return UnitOutcome::Complete(None); }`), `byroredux/src/npc_spawn/resumable/mod.rs:267-273` (`Complete(root: None)` returned to the caller), `byroredux/src/cell_loader/references/mod.rs:~765` (`if let Some(root) = result.root { … }` skips the whole identity/script/entity-count block), `crates/plugin/src/equip.rs:688-` (`ResolvedNpc` has terminals for traits/stats/factions/AI/inventory/spells/AI data/base data, and none for `Use Model/Animation`).
- **Status**: NEW. Related: #4137 (open, LOW; it records `0x0040` Model/Animation as "no consumer yet", with the premise that no CHARAL component depends on it - this is the gameplay consequence that premise missed), CHAR-SD-03 (sibling, same bit), #2567 (closed, built the creature arm).
- **Description**: FNV's wasteland fauna is placed as spawner shells (`VSpawnTier1CoyoteMed`, `VSpawnTier1GiantRatMed50`, `VSpawnSpecialTier3FireGeckoSmallNopah`, `LvlGecko`, …): `CREA` records with **no `MODL`**, a `TPLT` that points at a `LVLC`, and ACBS template flags with `0x0040` Use Model/Animation set. The engine takes the model, skeleton and animations from the leveled pick. This loader resolves every other template category through `ResolvedNpc` but has no Model/Animation terminal: `prepare_creature_state` reads `npc.model_path` of the shell, finds it empty, logs "spawning identity only" at DEBUG, and the Skeleton phase returns `Complete(None)`. The placement root made by `spawn_placement_root` (Transform, Name, ActorValues, FactionRanks, EditorPlacement, creature attack) is left behind with no body, and `finish_actor` (AI package, combat disposition, loot appearance, actor tagging) never runs. The placed REFR gets no `stamp_quest_reference`, no scripts and no `entity_count` increment. The log line is also wrong about what happens: nothing is "identity only".
- **Evidence** (throwaway read-only probes in `/tmp/audit/streaming-deep/fnv/`, FalloutNV.esm alone):
  - Independent raw-byte scan of the `CREA` group (`crea_raw.py`, no Rust parser): 1,578 records, 1,093 with `MODL`, **435 without `MODL` and with `TPLT`**, 50 with neither.
  - Parser view (`fnvprobe`, `EMPTY_CREA`): 485 model-less `CREA`; all 435 templated ones carry the `0x0040` flag; every one of them points at a `LVLC` (435 of 435; 371 of the lists have at least one directly modelled `CREA` entry, the rest nest).
  - Placements of the 435 templated model-less `CREA` bases: interior 413 + exterior 1,212 + persistent 252 = **1,877** (the other 50 model-less records are never placed). Placements of `CREA` bases that do have a model: 1,112 (interior 565, exterior 273, persistent 274). Share without a body: 1,877 / 2,989 = 63%.
  - `walk_inherited_records` already follows `LVLC` (`equip.rs:~600-640`), so the pick machinery exists; it is only never asked for the model.
  - `/tmp/audit/streaming-deep/fnv/probe/src/main.rs` (modes `EMPTY_CREA`, `TPL_KIND`) reproduces the numbers.
- **Disproof attempted**: (1) a different path supplies the model - `grep` for `0x0040`, `USE_MODEL`, `Model/Animation` finds only the `npc.rs` doc comment and unrelated constants; (2) the parser fills `model_path` from the template - the raw scan and the parser agree on 435; (3) the shells are never placed - 1,877 placements; (4) they are filtered as markers - the marker-model `CREA` (`marker_creature.nif`) are a different set of 33 records with 10 placements; (5) direct placements on `LVLN`/`LVLC` bases would bypass the shell - zero in FNV (`LEVELED_REFS` probe). Not verified: a live run (needs the engine); the orphan root's interaction with AI scans is read from the code only.
- **Impact**: Most of the wasteland's creature population (geckos, bighorners, rats, scorpions, coyotes, ants) is absent in exterior and interior cells alike. No combat targets, no ambient fauna, no `ACRE` quest creatures that are spawner shells. The 1,877 body-less roots still carry `ActorValues`/`FactionRanks`, which `spawn_placement_root`'s own comment calls a "phantom target for AI and combat scans" in the player-body case. It also means every FNV creature figure in the baseline entity counts excludes the majority of creatures.
- **Related**: FNV-SD-04 (the 1,112 that do spawn have no animation), #4137, CHAR-SD-03, #5495.
- **Suggested Fix**: add a `Use Model/Animation` terminal to `ResolvedNpc` (the same `resolve_inherited_record` walk with `0x0040`), and have `prepare_creature_state` derive skeleton/NIFZ/idle/walk from that terminal's `model_path` and `body_part_models`. Decide the LVLC pick policy explicitly (today: highest eligible level, last on a tie). Make the `Complete(None)` branch despawn the orphan root, or stamp identity, and fix the log text. Add a test with a model-less shell over a modelled template.

### FNV-SD-02: The worldspace persistent CELL is spawned once for the entry window; persistent refs beyond it never materialise as the player moves
- **Severity**: HIGH (fails under realistic conditions - continuous travel; the workaround, a door round trip that re-centres the window, is fragile)
- **Dimension**: 1 (cell load and streaming)
- **Location**: `byroredux/src/cell_loader/exterior.rs:1143-1151` (`for placed in &cell.references { if persistent_position_is_local(…) { local_refs.push } else if is_actor { remote_actor_refs.push } }`), `:1180-1189` (`persistent_position_is_local`, Chebyshev against `center_grid`), `byroredux/src/scene/world_setup.rs:1083-1092` (the only caller, guarded by `state.persistent_root.is_none() && state.persistent_apply.is_none()`), `byroredux/src/cell_loader/exterior.rs:370-406` (remote actors become identity-only stubs). No other site consumes `worldspace_persistent_cells` for spawning (grep: `barter.rs`, `asset_provider/script.rs`, `reference_state.rs` are census readers).
- **Status**: NEW. Searched open and closed issues for "persistent cell" (#5483, #3379, #3376, #2275, #4112, #5248 are about the job's stamping, budgeting, stubs and the corpse set, not the window). `AUDIT_EXTERIOR_2026-10-19.md` and `AUDIT_SCRIPTING_2026-10-19.md` re-read the persistent job and report it as holding; neither asks when it re-evaluates.
- **Description**: A worldspace's persistent CELL holds every reference flagged Persistent, wherever it stands in the world. `begin_worldspace_persistent_cell(wctx, center_grid, full_3d_radius, …)` partitions them once, at worldspace entry: refs within `radius_load` of the arrival cell are spawned by the persistent job; actors beyond it become `SceneAliasCandidate` stubs; **non-actor refs beyond it are dropped**. Nothing re-runs the partition when streaming moves the centre, and nothing promotes a stub to a full actor when its position enters the loaded radius. The persistent root is rebuilt only by a worldspace-level drain (door transition, `drain_streaming_state`), centred on the new arrival. The design doc describes this population as "resident … never evicted by radius streaming" (`stream-boundary-state-continuity.md` §1), which is true of the 3D that was spawned, not of the population.
- **Evidence** (`refs_near` example with an unbounded radius, `fnvprobe` mode `PERSIST_DOORS`, centre = the P0 arrival cell (-17, 0), radius 12):
  - WastelandNV persistent cell: **6,069 refs**; 884 within radius 12 (157 within radius 3); **5,185 beyond radius 12**.
  - Of the 6,069: **373 teleport doors** (335 beyond radius 12), 913 refs whose base carries a script (808 beyond), 312 item/container/activator/terminal refs, 530 creature refs (274 with a model, 256 model-less/marker). Among the 4,539 beyond-window refs with a known static base: 1,284 `MarkerXHeading`, 527 `marker_creature`, 233 `architecture\strip` pieces, 90 `HELIOS_One` pieces.
  - Beyond the window, concrete examples: `HeliosOneSolarReflectorRow` rows (grid 3, -1/0), `BMRadioControlPanel` ×4 and `NVBlackMtnSatelliteDishNOPHYSICS` (Black Mountain), `IndDoorSmAnim01` ×N (teleport-door industrial exteriors), `BlackCaveDoor`, `UtlDoor01`, `HVPaladinDead06`, `MoeTheCentaur`.
  - Other FNV worldspaces are small enough that radius 12 covers them (`thestripworldnew` 477 refs, `freesideworld` 172).
- **Disproof attempted**: (1) the exterior tile loader adds persistent refs by position - `ExteriorCellApplyJob`/`load_one_exterior_cell` read `cell.references` of the tile only, and the walker keeps the persistent CELL separate (`wrld.rs`, `index.rs:24-29`; `refs_near` itself documents "not the grid cell they stand in"); (2) a later system promotes stubs - `grep` for promote/materialize finds only `materialize_scene_actor_alias_stubs` (identity stubs); (3) the real engine does not load far persistent 3D either - the GECK says only that a persistent reference "must be checked for the reference to be used in a script" (local wiki `Reference.wiki`); I did not find a source that states the 3D-loading rule, so the claim is the Bethesda-engine behaviour as I know it (a persistent reference's 3D loads when the cell it stands in is in the loaded grid, while its data is always available), flagged as needing a ruling. (4) the door round trip re-centres the window: true and noted as the workaround. Not verified live.
- **Impact**: after a continuous walk from Goodsprings, far persistent doors cannot be activated, quest props and scripted activators are absent (their object scripts are also not attached, because attach happens at spawn), persistent creatures and the Helios/Black Mountain set pieces are missing. Combined with FNV-SD-01 and SCR-SD-03 (persistent ObScript refs on stubs run `GameMode` regardless) the persistent population is both over-scripted where it is stubbed and absent where it is not.
- **Related**: SCR-SD-03, #5997, #4112, EXT-D6-xx (separate), `stream-boundary-state-continuity.md`.
- **Suggested Fix**: keep the partition list on the job/state and run it as part of the per-step streaming reconcile: spawn the persistent refs (and promote stubbed actors) whose position enters `radius_load`, release them past `radius_unload`, owned by the persistent root or by the tile `CellRoot`. Pin it with a test that moves the centre.

### FNV-SD-03: XESP enable-parent children spawn in the wrong initial state for 56% of FNV children, and nothing cascades a parent's runtime enable/disable
- **Severity**: MEDIUM (incorrect world state with a workaround; arguably HIGH for quest-staged content)
- **Dimension**: 1 (reference gating)
- **Location**: `byroredux/src/cell_loader/references/mod.rs:500-517` (skip only when `EnableParent::default_disabled()`), `:640-649` (`reference_is_disabled(…, placed_ref.initially_disabled && placed_ref.enable_parent.is_none())`, the child's own flag is ignored when it has a parent), `crates/plugin/src/esm/cell/placed_ref.rs:466-490` (`default_disabled(self) = form_id != 0 && inverted`, "interim predicate … long-term fix is a two-pass loader"), `crates/scripting/src/fragment/state.rs:85-95` (`AuthoredDisabledReferences` repeats the rule, so `IsDisabled`/`IsEnabled` agree with the loader and are wrong the same way), `crates/scripting/src/obscript_actions.rs:25-28` ("the runtime has no enable-parent ledger to consult").
- **Status**: NEW (quantified). The mechanism is the intentional interim from closed #471 / #349; #4813 (closed) added the Initially-Disabled flag but explicitly leaves XESP children to "the heuristic above". No open issue.
- **Description**: A non-inverted XESP child is visible iff its parent is enabled. The loader assumes every parent is enabled, so every non-inverted child spawns and every inverted child is skipped. FNV authors **disabled "marker" parents** that quests enable later, with the staged content parented to them. The parent's own `Initially Disabled` flag (0x800) is decoded and honoured for the parent (#4813), but the child never consults it.
- **Evidence** (`fnvprobe`, a global FormID → flag table over interior, exterior and persistent cells; parents all resolve):
  - 3,337 XESP children in FalloutNV.esm. Engine-vs-truth: **1,876 spawn although their parent starts disabled** (exterior 768, persistent 354, interior 754; 540 of them actors) and **72 are skipped although they should be visible** (inverted child of a disabled parent: 6 + 22 + 44). 17 of the 1,876 have a parent that itself carries an XESP.
  - Raw-record check (`rec.py`): parent `0015D98C` (`VHDBattleEffectsMark…`) is `REFR flags=0xc00` (Persistent + Initially Disabled); its children `0015E4AF`/`0015E4B5` carry `XESP 8cd91500…`/`8dd91500…`, flags byte 0 (non-inverted). Those children are fires, `ImpactScorch02` decals, `Vault87Blood01` and `VHDDeadNCRTrooper` corpses of the Hoover Dam battle, all visible from a fresh start. Similar: the Strip light beams under `VOVWestsideLightStar…` (`0013A9ED`), Legion recruit corpses under `00152407`, smoke and fire FX (`DLC03FXDarkSmoke`, `FXSmokeMed01`, `FXFireMed01`) under `0016A270`.
  - Goodsprings area: of 36 XESP children within radius 12 of (-17, 0), 24 spawn wrongly (10 of 20 within radius 3). The Prospector saloon has 0 XESP children.
- **Disproof attempted**: (1) parents are enabled by default - the flag shows otherwise for the 1,876; (2) the child's own Initially-Disabled flag covers it - the loader drops that flag whenever an XESP exists (`initially_disabled && enable_parent.is_none()`, #4813's rule), and 784 of the 1,876 carry it, so even that signal is discarded; (3) nested chains invalidate the census - 17 of 1,876. Not verified live; the semantic "child follows the parent's enable state" is the GECK/CS Enable Parent definition, whose local pages I did not re-read for the Pop In bit.
- **Impact**: aftermath dressing, battle scenes, quest-gated NPCs and effect sets appear from the start of the game. Because `Enable()` on a parent does not cascade either, fixing only the initial state would hide them permanently when a script later enables the parent. Fix both.
- **Related**: #471, #4813, SCR-SD-03 (disabled placements still attach and run scripts), `reference_enable_gate_tests.rs`.
- **Suggested Fix**: build a FormID → (initially_disabled, enable_parent) table once per load order (the data `install_authored_disabled_references` already walks), evaluate parent state recursively with `ReferenceEnableState` overrides, and cascade a script `Enable`/`Disable` of a parent to its children.

- **Severity**: MEDIUM
- **Dimension**: 4/5 (animation at spawn)
- **Location**: `byroredux/src/npc_spawn.rs:577` (`creature_idle_kf_path`), `:943` (`creature_walk_kf_path`), `byroredux/src/npc_spawn/resumable/runtime.rs:472-473` (both derived for every creature), Finalize at `:~884-935` (idle None → "spawning unanimated"; walk None → no `WalkAnimation`). ### FNV-SD-05: 203 FNV `NPC_` corpses carry an authored 18-entry `XRGD` pose that the loader neither parks nor applies
- **Severity**: MEDIUM
- **Dimension**: 2/4 (authored corpses at spawn)
- **Location**: `byroredux/src/cell_loader/references/mod.rs:772-787` (the pose is parked only when `placed_ref.starts_dead`, a flag the plugin sets for the Skyrim/FO4 24-byte-header family only), `byroredux/src/ragdoll_pose.rs:64-85` (`HUMANOID_RAGDOLL_BODIES`, Skyrim bone names), `byroredux/src/combat.rs:1114-1124` (consumer).
- **Status**: Existing: #5015 (open; its body already says "one XRGD decode serves both issues" and scopes FO3/FNV in prose, but the title and census are Skyrim/FO4). The FNV leg is quantified here.
- **Evidence**: `corpse_marker_census` on FalloutNV.esm: 387 `XRGD` refs, 380 of them rule-dead (`XRGD & rule-dead`), 89 rule-dead corpses without `XRGD`. `xrgd_dump`: 203 `NPC_` placements carry a pose, **all with exactly 18 entries**, which is FNV's humanoid ragdoll body count (baseline: 18 bodies / 17 joints). The pose is data-compatible in shape; what is missing is the FNV entry→body order (not sourced: the Skyrim order was derived empirically) and a trigger that does not depend on `starts_dead`.
- **Impact**: ~250 corpse bases stand upright and ragdoll-collapse from the idle pose instead of lying where authored; bodies placed on ledges or over props fall. Same class as the Skyrim/FO4 impact in #5015.
- **Related**: #5015, #5005, PHYS-SD-02, GAME-SD-03 (corpse position lost across eviction), the uncommitted `pose_bone_id` WIP in the working tree.
- **Suggested Fix**: extend #5015's scope to FO3/FNV: park the pose for base-dead FO3/FNV actors, derive the entry order from the FNV skeleton, and gate on a per-skeleton body-name table rather than the Skyrim list.

### FNV-SD-06: `PSDT` Day-of-Week is ignored; 53 FNV packages (5 actor bases) schedule by weekday
- **Severity**: LOW
- **Dimension**: 5 (ambient AI package selection)
- **Location**: `crates/plugin/src/esm/records/misc/pack.rs:355-358, 406` (`PackSchedule` keeps start hour and duration only; the PTDT doc at `:149-160` calls the other PSDT bytes "unused"), `byroredux/src/npc_spawn/ai_package.rs:772-791` (`select_active_package` passes only `game_hour`).
- **Status**: NEW. ROADMAP lists "calendar-aware scheduling" as an open M42 item generically; no issue. The calendar exists now (`GameTimeRes::day`, `GameCalendar`, `gregorian_weekday`).

All three variants pass the hour test, so the first listed wins every day.
- **Impact**: the Primm Prospector Den patrol and the Goodsprings gecko hunters run the wrong day's route. Small, but the docs call the bytes unused and they are not.
