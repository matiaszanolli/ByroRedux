# ESM / Plugin Parser Audit — 2026-08-13

**Command**: `/audit-esm` (all 8 dimensions, `--depth deep`, all 7 games)
**Subject**: `crates/plugin/` — 40,687 LOC / 71 files, the second-largest crate in
the workspace and, until this pass, **the largest subsystem with no owner audit**.
**Tree**: dimensions measured against `main` @ `c582f170` / `0c129879`; findings
re-verified against `205744ae` after a mid-audit checkout change — see
*Provenance* at the end of this report.
**Baseline**: `cargo test -p byroredux-plugin` → **687 passed, 0 failed, 23 ignored**
(re-verified by every dimension after scratch cleanup; unchanged by this audit).
**Dedup basis**: 200 open issues + every `docs/audits/AUDIT_{FNV,FO3,SKYRIM,FO4,OBLIVION,STARFIELD}_*.md`.
There is no prior `AUDIT_ESM_*` report; this crate's findings have historically
lived in the per-game reports, so that is where duplicates were hunted.

> **First audit of the parser *as a parser*.** Per-game audits each sample one
> game's slice. Nothing had ever audited the GRUP walker, sub-record byte
> accounting, the FormID remap's *reach*, record-schema dispatch coverage, the
> CELL/WRLD walkers, the localized-strings loader, or the `EsmIndex → ECS`
> handoff as a whole. Seven of the eight dimensions found HIGH-severity defects
> in vanilla content paths.

### What was actually parsed

**All seven games, from disk, end to end** — `Oblivion.esm`, `Fallout3.esm`,
`FalloutNV.esm`, `Skyrim.esm` (SE), `Fallout4.esm`, `SeventySix.esm`,
`Starfield.esm`, plus DLC masters (`Dawnguard`, `Dragonborn`, `DLCCoast`,
`DLCNukaWorld`, `DLCRobot`, `NW.esm`, `Anchorage`, `ShatteredSpace`) and nine
real localized companion files extracted from shipped BSA/BA2 archives.
**13.4 million records walked.** Walk completeness was validated three ways:
100 % top-level byte coverage on all seven masters, an independent Python walker
reproducing the Rust counts byte-identically on two masters, and exact agreement
with the checked-in Starfield Phase-0 baseline.

This is not a code-reading audit. Nearly every finding below is backed by shipped
bytes, a measured census, or a reverted throwaway probe.

### The five themes

**1. Decode coverage falls off a cliff at FO4 (89.0 %) and Starfield (90.0 %)**
after sitting flat at 97–99 % for Oblivion→Skyrim. That is not gradual schema
drift — it is three specific structural losses: the FO4+/Starfield dialogue tree
nested under `QUST` and discarded (ESM-D4-02), the un-dispatched long tail
(ESM-D4-05), and the actor-record schema cliff (ESM-D8-04).

**2. `merge_from` is incomplete at two nesting levels, and the engine only ever
uses the merged path.** `EsmIndex::merge_from` drops 41 of 92 maps
(ESM-D4-01 / ESM-D7-01 — found independently by two dimensions), and
`EsmCellIndex::merge_from` drops `landscape` (ESM-D5-02). Because
`parse_record_indexes_in_load_order` seeds from `default()` and folds *every*
plugin through `merge_from` — including single-plugin loads — those 41 maps are
**empty in every index the engine has ever built**. Every integration test calls
`parse_esm` directly, which is why 687 green tests coexist with this.

**3. The FormID remap machinery is correct; its *reach* is not.** Every
`EsmIndex` map **key** is remapped at a single choke point. But only 2 of ~20
embedded FormID **values** in the REFR walker are, and only 12 of ~60 typed
parsers ever receive a remap. Under any non-identity load order — the normal case
from the second DLC onward, or with any ESL — the two spaces silently stop
intersecting: DLC doors are dead, cell lighting falls through, landscape textures
resolve to nothing (ESM-D3-01/02/03).

**4. Localized text is 100 % unresolved on every shipping game.** The `.STRINGS`
loader is loose-file-only, and no Bethesda game ships `Data/Strings/` — all pack
them in BSA/BA2. Measured: 9,225/9,225 Skyrim, 5,295/5,295 FO4, 6,830/6,830
Starfield names render as `<lstring 0x…>` (ESM-D6-01).

**5. Four decoders produce confidently wrong numbers from correctly-sized
payloads.** `GLOB.FLTV` read as int (204/218 FNV globals wrong, feeding every
CTDA comparand at runtime), `CONT.DATA` field order inverted (100 % of containers,
all four masters), FNV `ARMO.DNAM` DT always 0.0, FO4 `ARMO.DATA` transposed.
None is a panic; all parse "successfully". Every byte-layout test in the crate
builds its fixture from the parser's own assumption, so none could ever have
caught them (ESM-D2-07).

### Cost, measured for the first time

ESM parse cost had **no owner** — `/audit-performance` Dim 8 owns NIF parse cost
only. Measured (`--release`, warm cache, Ryzen 7950X): 1.25–4.95 s wall and
0.8–3.2 GB peak RSS per master, at **2.1–5.0× file size**. A live `EsmIndex` holds
**1,156 MB** (Skyrim SE + 4 DLC) or **1,381 MB** (FO4 + 6 DLC) of non-evictable
session RAM — the engine's single largest RAM consumer, absent from
`docs/engine/memory-budget.md`, uncapped, never evicted. And every interior door
transition re-parses the entire load order: **3.07 s / 1.29 GB peak**, against an
inline comment claiming "~1 s extra".

## Deduplication & Reconciliation

Two dimensions reached the same defect, and two appeared to contradict each other.
Both are resolved here rather than left for the reader.

**ESM-D4-01 ≡ ESM-D7-01 — merged.** Dimensions 4 and 7 independently found
`EsmIndex::merge_from` dropping 41 of 92 maps, from opposite directions (dispatch
coverage vs. the ECS handoff). Dim 4 identified the field diff; Dim 7 measured the
loss empirically (7,011 records per `Skyrim.esm`) and traced the live victim
(`populate_havok_idle_runtime`, 3,285 IDLE records → 0). Filed once, below, with
both bodies of evidence. Independent rediscovery is corroboration, not noise.

**ESM-D1-01 vs ESM-D4-10 — apparent contradiction, both correct.** Dim 1 reported
a HIGH: FO4 HEDR `0.95` mis-bands to `Fallout3NV`. Dim 4 reported "bands
themselves verified correct against all six masters — **no band bug**." Both
statements are true of what each sampled, and neither alone is accurate.
Independently verified during the merge:

```
Fallout4.esm     HEDR=1.00  -> Fallout4    (correct; this is all Dim 4's six-master sweep saw)
DLCRobot.esm     HEDR=1.00  -> Fallout4    (correct)
DLCworkshop01    HEDR=1.00  -> Fallout4    (correct)
DLCCoast.esm     HEDR=0.95  -> Fallout3NV  <- WRONG
DLCNukaWorld     HEDR=0.95  -> Fallout3NV  <- WRONG
DLCworkshop02    HEDR=0.95  -> Fallout3NV  <- WRONG
DLCworkshop03    HEDR=0.95  -> Fallout3NV  <- WRONG
DLCUltraHighRes  HEDR=0.95  -> Fallout3NV  <- WRONG
```

**The base master classifies correctly; 5 of 7 FO4 DLC masters do not**, plus 29
of 118 installed FO4 plugins. Dim 4's contribution stands on its own terms and is
retained as ESM-D4-10: the `from_header` comment's claim that a misclassification
would be *latent* is now stale, because `parse_armo` gates the MODL/model-path
split on `GameKind::Fallout4`.

**Not re-filed** (verified as deliberate, documented behaviour): #1308 / the
Oblivion `0x01` standalone-authoring pass-through; #2698 (`XPRI`, adjacent to
ESM-D3-01, same file — fix together); `actor_value_derive.rs` formulas (owned by
`/audit-character` Dim 4).

### One incomplete item, stated plainly

Dimension 8's report references an **ESM-D8-05** in its §2.2 `WEAP` row that it
never wrote — the agent was terminated mid-file. What is independently verifiable:
`WEAP.DNAM` has exactly one production decode arm
(`crates/plugin/src/esm/records/items.rs:207`), gated to `GameKind::Fallout3NV`,
which reads `anim_type`@0 and `min_spread`@16 and explicitly skips bytes 1–15;
the arm's own comment states Skyrim's ~100-byte rewrite "isn't decoded yet". That
is consistent with the `WEAP` unknown-sub-code curve in the matrix, but the
finding as Dim 8 intended it does not exist and has not been reconstructed.

## Findings — HIGH (10)

### ESM-D1-01: FO4 HEDR `0.95` mis-bands to `GameKind::Fallout3NV`
**Dim 1** · `TES4`/`HEDR` · `crates/plugin/src/esm/reader.rs:141-159`, `:103`

`from_header` routes `0.98..=1.04` → `Fallout4` and `0.93..=0.95` → `Fallout3NV`.
FO4 ships **two** HEDR versions in vanilla and the CK stamps `0.95` on a large
fraction of third-party plugins. Verified end-to-end on real files: `DLCCoast.esm`
→ `Fallout3NV`; 5 of 7 FO4 DLC masters and 29 of 118 installed FO4 plugins carry
`0.95`. The enum's own doc says `/// Fallout 4 (HEDR 0.95)` — doc and band
disagree, and the band is what runs.

**No longer latent.** Since #439 was written, `GameKind::Fallout4` acquired splits
`Fallout3NV` does not share: `ACBS` NPC layout, `WTHR` `NAM0`/`FNAM`, the
`is_fo4_plus` gate that **skips `PKIN`/`MOVS`/`MSWP` GRUPs wholesale**, biped
slots, CHARAL ruleset selection, baked `.bto` LOD, and FO4 light-animation flags.
`merge_from` is `self.game = other.game` (last plugin wins) and the load-order
builder parses the target plugin last — so **one `0.95` mod ESP relabels the whole
merged index**.
**Fix**: tighten to `0.945..=0.955 → Fallout4` (0.94999998 vs 0.94 separate
cleanly in f32) *and* add a corroborating discriminator (TES4 `version` u16 at
offset 20-21, or `TES4.TNAM`/`ONAM` presence). Pin the 0.95 case against
`DLCCoast.esm`.

### ESM-D2-01: `GLOB.FLTV` is always a 4-byte float on disk; the parser reinterprets it as `i16`/`i32`
**Dim 2** · `GLOB`/`FNAM`,`FLTV` · `crates/plugin/src/esm/records/global.rs:68-79`

`FNAM` is a *display/rounding* hint (xEdit `wbFloat(FLTV,'Value')`, unchanged
TES4→TES5); the CK stores short and long globals as floats. Switching the decode
type on it reads two bytes of a mantissa or a raw bit pattern as an integer.

### ESM-D3-01: REFR `XTEL` / `XESP` FormIDs are never load-order remapped
**Dim 3** · `REFR`/`XTEL`,`XESP` (+`XLKR`,`XPOD`,`XRMR`,`XTXR`,`XEMI`,`XMSP`,`XOWN`,`XGLB`) · `crates/plugin/src/esm/cell/walkers.rs:702-870`

### ESM-D5-02: An override CELL replaces the base tile's LANDSCAPE wholesale
**Dim 5** · `CELL`/`LAND` · `crates/plugin/src/esm/cell/mod.rs:1082-1134`

#1546 established that a Bethesda override CELL is a *partial* record and fixed
`references` to merge per-REFR. The same reasoning was never applied to the other
per-cell payloads: `merge_from` inserts `over_cell` verbatim after merging only
`references`, so `landscape`, `navmeshes`, `precombined_mesh_hashes` and
`absorbed_refs` are replaced by whatever the override carries — `None` for a
partial override.

| base ← override | tiles where base has `landscape` and the override does not |
|---|---|
| `Skyrim.esm` ← `Dawnguard.esm` | **218** |
| `Skyrim.esm` ← `Dragonborn.esm` | **29** |
| `Fallout4.esm` ← `DLCCoast.esm` | **144** |

The exterior spawner gates terrain on `if let Some(ref land) = cell.landscape`, so
a cleared landscape is a **hole in the world** — no heightmap, no splat layers.
This is the documented `--master Skyrim.esm --esm Dawnguard.esm` invocation.
`absorbed_refs` clearing is the FO4 mirror: an override omitting `XCRI`/`XPRI`
un-absorbs the precombine set and re-spawns geometry already baked into the
`_oc.nif` (doubled geometry + z-fighting). Eight `merge_from_*` tests exist; none
covers `landscape`, `navmeshes` or `absorbed_refs`.
**Fix**: carry forward the base's payloads when the override authors none — the
same partial-record rule `references` already follows.

### ESM-D5-03: Interior cells are keyed by EditorID only — 231 vanilla FO4 interiors and 6,114 child records are discarded
**Dim 5** · `CELL`/`EDID` · `crates/plugin/src/esm/cell/walkers.rs:554-593`

`parse_cell_group` inserts an interior cell only when

|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `Fallout4.esm` | 1,195 | **231** | **964** |
| `Skyrim.esm` / `FalloutNV.esm` / `Oblivion.esm` / `Starfield.esm` | 590 / 388 / 1,855 / 11,985 | 0 | all |

### ESM-D6-01: `.STRINGS` discovery is loose-file-only — every localized name is a placeholder on 100 % of real installs
**Dim 6** · `FULL`/`DESC`/`NAM1` · `crates/plugin/src/esm/strings_table.rs:214-245`

`StringTableSet::load` resolves companion files exclusively through
`std::fs::read(<plugin_dir>/Strings/…)`. **No installed Bethesda game ships a loose
`Data/Strings/`** — all four keep the strings in a BSA/BA2. So `load()` returns an
all-`None` set and every lstring falls through to `<lstring 0xNNNNNNNN>`.

| Master | ITEM+NPC names | unresolved, as shipped | unresolved, with extracted tables |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `Skyrim.esm` | 9,225 | **9,225 (100 %)** | 0 |
| `Fallout4.esm` | 5,295 | **5,295 (100 %)** | 0 |
| `Starfield.esm` | 6,830 | **6,830 (100 %)** | 85 (1.2 %) |

The table parser itself is **correct** — nine real companion files parsed at 100 %
entry recovery with zero slack. It is the *discovery* that is broken. #1553 closed

**Fix**: give `load` an archive-backed source (the cell loader already holds a

### ESM-D7-03: Every interior door transition re-parses the entire load order — 3.07 s / 1.29 GB, against an inline claim of "~1 s extra"
**Dim 7** · `byroredux/src/cell_loader/load.rs:305-310`, driven from `transition.rs:385`

`load_cell_with_masters` calls `parse_record_indexes_in_load_order` unconditionally
and nothing caches the result, so every door walk rebuilds the index from raw ESM
bytes. Measured with `/usr/bin/time -v` over the exact plugin list and sequence,
release, warm cache: **3.07 s wall, 1,287,792 kB peak**. That is the *whole* parse,
not "extra", and 3× the stated figure. The claim also predates the multi-master
path — cost scales with load-order length (96,324 records single-plugin → 114,608
for the five-plugin order), not with the cell being entered. `ExteriorWorldContext`
holds `Arc<EsmIndex>` with no teardown, so the previous index is still reachable
while the new one is built (a plausible ~2.3 GB two-index peak, not confirmable
without launching a windowed engine).
**Fix**: hoist the parsed `Arc<EsmIndex>` into a session-scoped resource keyed by
the load-order fingerprint; the interior path then costs a `HashMap` lookup. At
minimum correct the comment to the measured numbers.

## Method & Hygiene

- **Eight dimensions, max 3 concurrent**, each a fresh agent with the full
  checklist, the dedup corpus, and an explicit instruction to disprove its own
  findings before keeping them.
- **No windowed engine instance was launched** by any dimension
  (`feedback_no_parallel_engine_launch`). `--sf-smoke` is a headless path that
  returns before Vulkan init.
- Throwaway probes were written under `crates/plugin/examples/` and
  `crates/plugin/src/esm/records/tests.rs`, run, and reverted. Working tree clean;
  baseline re-verified at **687 passed / 0 failed / 23 ignored** after cleanup.
- **Dimension 8 was terminated mid-write** when its host process exited. Its
  report survived on disk with the coverage matrix, the cost tables and four of
  five findings intact; the missing ESM-D8-05 is documented above as unreconstructed
  rather than guessed at.
- Several suspected findings were **disproved and recorded so they are not
  re-litigated**: Oblivion's 24-byte `LIGH.DATA` (bytes 16–23 are zero in all 561 —
  the short form truncates after FOV, decode is correct); `rgb_color`/`rgba_color`
  swaps (all 16 sites are 4-byte RGBA); null-terminator overruns; direct-slice
  panics (~60 sites, all correctly guarded — the bugs here are *wrong offsets under
  a correct guard*, a more expensive failure mode); the ESL 12-bit mask collapse
  (both masks are `0x0FFF` and pinned by tests); `StringsTable::parse`'s bounds
  ordering (checks precede `with_capacity`, unlike Dimension 1's defect).

(Dims 1–7). By the time the merge ran, the working copy had been changed to
`205744ae`, which is **not** an ancestor of `c582f170` — a divergent state whose
