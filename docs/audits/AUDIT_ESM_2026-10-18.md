## Findings

### MEDIUM

#### ESM-2026-10-18-EXT-01: LTEX to TXST path and texture-set resolution runs per plugin, so a DLC LTEX that points at a master TXST never resolves
- **Severity**: MEDIUM. A vanilla DLC loses terrain layers; the loader logs "not in landscape_textures map; skipping layer".
- **Dimension**: ESM→ECS Handoff / FormID & Load Order
- **Record / Sub-record**: `LTEX` / `TNAM`
- **Location**:
  - `crates/plugin/src/esm/records/parse.rs:472-476`: `landscape_textures` is filled from `ltex_to_txst` joined with this plugin's own `txst_textures`
  - `crates/plugin/src/esm/records/parse.rs:487-492`: `landscape_texture_sets`, same join against this plugin's own `texture_sets`
  - `crates/plugin/src/esm/cell/index.rs:509-513`: `merge_from` only `extend`s the already-resolved maps
- **Status**: NEW. Not in the 119-issue snapshot. #3314 (closed) fixed the FormID remap of the `TNAM` value, not this join.
- **Description**: `ltex_to_txst` is a local of `parse_esm_with_load_order`. It is joined against the same plugin's TXST maps and then dropped, and it is not a field of `EsmCellIndex`. A plugin whose LTEX references a TXST authored in a master therefore produces no `landscape_textures` or `landscape_texture_sets` entry at all. The merge cannot repair it. An override that re-points `TNAM` at a different master TXST would also leave the master's stale path in place.
- **Evidence** (my census, `ltex_dlc.py`, `ltex_cmp.py`, `hh_land.py`):
  - `HonestHearts.esm` has 6 LTEX. `0x0100CD37 ScrubBarrenWastes02GrassMix` has `TNAM = 0x000357BD`, a `FalloutNV.esm` TXST. Its own LAND records use it in 550 `ATXT` layers.
  - `Update.esm` has 15 LTEX, 13 of which point at `Skyrim.esm` TXSTs. All 13 are no-op overrides, identical to the master's `TNAM`. Only the two that point at Update's own TXST change anything, and they resolve locally.
  - So the defect is live on real DLC data; Skyrim only escapes because the overrides happen to be identical.
- **Impact**: Fallout New Vegas Honest Hearts (Zion) drops about 550 splat layers of `ScrubBarrenWastes02GrassMix`. Any mod LTEX that reuses a vanilla TXST (the common authoring pattern) renders with a missing terrain layer.
- **Related**: #3314, #5075-class "keyed in global space" invariants.
- **Suggested Fix**: keep `ltex_to_txst` (a `HashMap<u32, u32>`) on `EsmCellIndex`, merge it last-writer-wins in `merge_from`, and run the TXST join once after the final merge. Pin it with a two-plugin test where the LTEX is in the DLC and the TXST is in the master.

#### ESM-2026-10-18-EXT-02: WTHR `PNAM` cloud colours are read with a fixed 4-slot layer stride; FNV (6 slots) and FO4/FO76/Starfield (8 slots) read the wrong bytes for layers 1 to 3
- **Severity**: MEDIUM. The tint is multiplied into the cloud dome, so a wrong table is a visible error on the reference title.
- **Dimension**: Sub-Record Byte Accounting
- **Record / Sub-record**: `WTHR` / `PNAM`
- **Location**: `crates/plugin/src/esm/records/weather.rs:844-859` (`parse_cloud_colors`); callers at `:667` (non-Skyrim arm) and `:1031` (Skyrim arm)
- **Status**: NEW
- **Description**: xEdit's `wbWeatherCloudColors` is an array of `wbWeatherTimeOfDay('Layer')` structs. `wbWeatherTimeOfDay` (`wbDefinitionsCommon.pas`) has 6 colours per layer on FNV (4 when the sub-record size is 64 or 160, via `wbWeatherTimeOfDayDecider`), 8 on FO4/FO76/Starfield, and 4 elsewhere. The code always uses `offset = (layer * 4 + slot) * 4`, a 16-byte layer stride. That is only right for Skyrim, FO3 and short FNV records. FNV `NAM0` already honours the 240/160 split (`:505-528`), but `PNAM` does not.
- **Evidence** (real data, `FalloutNV.esm`):
  - `PNAM` is 96 B on 55 of 63 weathers (6 slots × 4 layers) and 64 B on 8.
  - FO4 `PNAM` is 1,024 B on 67 of 71 records (32 layers × 8 slots), and FO76 is 1,024 B on 123 of 123.
  - `NVWastelandClearEast` `PNAM`, 24 RGBA words. Layer 3 (words 18 to 23) reads as a coherent sunrise/day/sunset/night/noon/midnight ramp: `(230,190,182) (241,238,228) (253,203,189) (24,28,35) (241,238,228) (37,44,56)`.
  - The code's layer 1 reads words 4 to 7: `(255,0,128) (0,0,0) (255,255,255) (255,255,255)`. That means a Day tint of black and a Sunrise tint of magenta. The code's layer 2 (words 8 to 11) also has a black Day.
  - `parse_weather_dynamics_keeps_timing_cloud_motion_and_lightning` (`weather.rs:1915`) builds a 64-byte `PNAM` and never exercises the 96-byte form.
- **Impact**: 55 of 63 FNV weathers and about 65 of 71 FO4 weathers feed wrong cloud tints for layers 1 to 3 into `cloud_tints` (`env_translate/sky_weather.rs:267`, `systems/weather.rs:505`). Layer 0 is correct.
- **Related**: EXT-04 (the same non-Skyrim branch never reads FO4 cloud textures).
- **Suggested Fix**: derive slots-per-layer from `(game, data.len())`. FNV: 4 when the length is 64, else 6. FO4/FO76/Starfield: 8 when the length is 1,024. Skyrim: 4. Pin it with the real 96-byte FNV words above.

#### ESM-2026-10-18-EXT-03: Skyrim `NAM0` and `DALC` are accepted only at their newest size, so older-version vanilla weathers decode to no colours
- **Severity**: MEDIUM. Wrong parse of vanilla records. No live consumer today, because the FX weathers are reachable only through the unconsumed `sky_region` (#5463).
- **Dimension**: Sub-Record Byte Accounting
- **Record / Sub-record**: `WTHR` / `NAM0`, `DALC`
- **Location**: `weather.rs:960` (`NAM0 if len >= 17*4*4`), `:1069` (`DALC if len >= 32`)
- **Status**: NEW
- **Description**: xEdit gates the Skyrim `NAM0` groups on the record form version (`wbFromVersion` 31 for `Cloud LOD Diffuse`/`Cloud LOD Ambient`/`Fog Far`, 35 for `Sky Statics`, 37 for `Water Multiplier`/`Sun Glare`/`Moon Glare`). A record saved at version 35/36 carries 14 groups (224 B), and one at version 31 to 34 carries 13 (208 B). The Skyrim arm drops the whole sub-record unless it is at least 272 B. The Creation-era arm (`creation_nam0_shape`) is exact-size for FO4; the Skyrim arm has no equivalent.
- **Evidence**: `Skyrim.esm` has 11 weathers with `NAM0` = 224 B: `FXWthrInvertWindowsWindhelm`, `…Windhelm2`, `…Whiterun`, `FXWthrInvertLightsSolitude`, `…Whiterun`, `FXWthrSunlight`, `FXWthrSunlightWhite`, `FXWthrInvertDayNighWarm`, `FXWthrCaveBluePaleLight`, `FXWthrCaveBlueSkylight`, `FXWthrInvertDayNight`. `TESTCloudyRain` has `NAM0` = 208 B and `DALC` = 24 B ×4. All of them decode to an all-zero `sky_colors` table.
- **Impact**: zero today (see Severity). It becomes live when `CellData::sky_region` gains a consumer, because Skyrim interior window-light effects take their colours from exactly these weathers.
- **Suggested Fix**: decode `n = len / 16` groups for the Skyrim arm (13 to 17), leaving later groups at default, and accept `DALC` at 24 B (6 axes only, no fresnel). Pin with the 224-byte shape.

#### ESM-2026-10-18-EXT-04: FO4 / FO76 / Starfield WTHR cloud textures are never decoded
- **Severity**: MEDIUM. All four cloud layers are `None` on every Creation-era weather.
- **Dimension**: Record Schema Dispatch
- **Record / Sub-record**: `WTHR` / `00TX`..`O0TX`
- **Location**: `weather.rs:444-694` (`parse_wthr`). Only Skyrim is routed to `parse_wthr_skyrim`, and the generic arm matches `DNAM`/`CNAM`/`ANAM`/`BNAM` only.
- **Status**: NEW
- **Description**: FO4, FO76 and Starfield use the Skyrim-style `xTX` cloud list (`wbWeatherCloudTextures` for non-FO3 games). The generic branch has no arm for it, and `parse_wthr_00tx_fourccs_are_inert` pins the inert behaviour for FO3/FNV only. `byroredux/src/scene/world_setup.rs:354-375` then resolves `cloud_textures[0..4]` as `None`.
- **Evidence**: FO4 census has `00TX` on 63 of 71 weathers, FO76 on 123 of 123, Starfield on 0 of 3 (it uses `WTHS`). `grep` shows no `xTX` handling outside `parse_wthr_skyrim`.
- **Impact**: FO4/FO76 exteriors get no authored cloud layers. These are later-roadmap games, but the decode gap is silent.
- **Related**: EXT-02, EXT-05.
- **Suggested Fix**: route FO4/FO76/Starfield `xTX` through the shared `skyrim_cloud_layer_index`, after EXT-05 fixes the index map, and fill `cloud_textures[0..4]`.

### LOW

#### ESM-2026-10-18-EXT-05: the Skyrim cloud-layer FourCC index treats `'A'` as layer 10, but the real sequence is the ASCII run `'0'..'9', ':'..'@', 'A'..'O'`
- **Severity**: LOW. The full 32-slot table has no non-test consumer. Layers 0 to 9 (digits) are right, so the four layers the renderer uses are unaffected.
- **Dimension**: Sub-Record Byte Accounting
- **Record / Sub-record**: `WTHR` / `:0TX`..`O0TX`
- **Location**: `weather.rs:879-888` (`skyrim_cloud_layer_index`); test `weather.rs:1960` pins `A0TX` as layer 10 and `V0TX` as layer 31
- **Status**: NEW
- **Description**: xEdit's list is `_30_0TX`..`_39_0TX` (layers 0 to 9), `_3A_0TX`..`_40_0TX` (layers 10 to 16, the bytes `':' ';' '<' '=' '>' '?' '@'`), then `A0TX` = layer 17 up to `O0TX` = layer 31. The code maps only digits and `'A'..='V'`, with `'A'` = 10. So layers 10 to 16 are dropped and layers 17 to 28 are stored 7 slots too low. `'V'` does not exist on the wire.
- **Evidence**: census shows `:0TX`..`@0TX` present on Skyrim weathers (`:0TX` ×49 records, `@0TX` ×23, and so on), `A0TX`..`L0TX` present and `M0TX`..`O0TX` absent, consistent with `LNAM Max Cloud Layers = 29` (10 + 7 + 12).
- **Suggested Fix**: index = `byte - b'0'` for the first byte of the FourCC, with the ASCII range ending at `'O'`; update the test.

#### ESM-2026-10-18-EXT-06: Oblivion cloud decode: `DNAM`/`CNAM` layer order is the reverse of the record's own names, and the authored cloud speeds in `DATA[1..2]` are dropped
- **Severity**: LOW. Visual fidelity only; the sky falls back to the synthetic wind-driven drift.
- **Dimension**: Sub-Record Byte Accounting
- **Record / Sub-record**: `WTHR` / `CNAM`, `DNAM`, `DATA`
- **Location**: `weather.rs:684-685` (`DNAM`→layer 0, `CNAM`→layer 1), `:771` (the #4015 note on `DATA` bytes 1 to 2)
- **Status**: NEW
- **Description**: `wbDefinitionsTES4.pas:3585-3587` and the local UESP page define `CNAM` = Lower Cloud Layer and `DNAM` = Upper Cloud Layer, and `DATA[1]`/`DATA[2]` = Cloud Speed (Lower)/(Upper). The #4015 comment ("no source identifies bytes 1 to 2") is true for FO3/FNV, where xEdit marks them `wbUnused(2)`, but not for Oblivion. I could not verify which layer index the renderer treats as "lowest", so the order finding is partly unverified.
- **Evidence**: all 37 Oblivion weathers author non-zero speeds in those bytes (for example 42/19, 50/25, 101/63, 255/134), while `cloud_layer_velocities_authored` stays `false` for them.
- **Suggested Fix**: for `GameKind::Oblivion` map `CNAM`→0, `DNAM`→1 once the layer-order semantics are confirmed, and read `DATA[1..2]` into `cloud_layer_velocities` with `authored = true`.

- **WRLD**: `WNAM`/`PNAM`/`CNAM`/`NAM2`/`NAM3`/`NAM4`/`DNAM`/`NAM0`/`NAM9`/`ZNAM`/`INAM` widths match xEdit on all games. FO3 `PNAM` upper bits (`0xFFC4`) are editor junk, but every consumer masks single bits. The Oblivion inherit-all stamp is the only copy. `DNAM` water-height values of 9,999,999 / -500,000 on Skyrim cave worldspaces pass the 1e9 gate but are shadowed by per-cell `XCLW` (16,978 of 16,978 Skyrim exterior cells author one).
- **LAND**: `VHGT` 1,096 B, `VNML`/`VCLR` 3,267 B, `BTXT`/`ATXT` 8 B, `VTXT` 8-B rows, on all five games. `VHGT` accumulator matches the UESP algorithm. LAND records without `VHGT` (1,034 on Oblivion, 407 FNV, 154 Skyrim, 34 FO4) decode as flat zero-height terrain, which is the engine's own default.
- **XCLW tri-state**: sentinels `3.4e38`, `-3.4e38`, `-2147483648` and `4294953216` all fall to `None` under the 1e9 gate.
- **FormID remap** on every exterior field read: WRLD (`read_form_id`), CELL (`XCLR`, `XCWT`, `XCIM`, `XCMO`, `XCCM`, `XLCN`, `LTMP`), LAND (`BTXT`/`ATXT`), LTEX (`TNAM`/`GNAM`), WTHR (`IAD`, `IMSP`, `MNAM`, `NNAM`), CLMT (`WLST`/`WSLT`), REGN (post-pass), WATR (`TNAM`).
- **CLMT**: `WLST` strides (8 B Oblivion, 12 B elsewhere) match all census sizes, and Starfield `WSLT` is 12-B rows.
- **GRAS** `DATA` (32 B) matches xEdit FO3/FO4 field for field.
- **REGN** `RDWT` stride validation is correct for the ambiguous 24/48-byte payloads.
- **XCCM**: no vanilla exterior cell authors one, and the TES5/FO4 REGN reading is pinned by `parse_cell_tes5_fo4_xccm_is_a_region_not_a_climate`.
- **`decode_legacy_head` (#5796)**: equivalent to the four decoders it replaced.
- **Starfield `WTHS`**: untouched since the baseline, and `hostile_self_referential_diffs_decline_at_the_depth_cap` is green.
