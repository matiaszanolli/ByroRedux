### Dimension 4 — ESM architecture records + cell expansion

#### FO4-2026-10-27-01: FO4 "Use Land Data" child worldspaces are excluded from parent-landscape inheritance
- **Severity**: MEDIUM. This matches the class severity of #6089 and #5505.
- **Dimension**: 4. These are FO4 WRLD/CELL data through the shared exterior post-parse step. The mechanism belongs to `/audit-exterior`.
- **Location**:
  - `byroredux/src/cell_loader/load_order.rs:607-612`: `needs_exterior_processing` matches `Oblivion | Fallout3NV` only.
  - `load_order.rs:624-631`: `apply_exterior_processing`.
  - `load_order.rs:710`: `exterior_ready`, which uses the same predicate (#6407).
  - `crates/plugin/src/esm/cell/index.rs:525-529`: the `inherit_parent_landscapes` doc says the caller gates on the game.
- **Status**: NEW. This is the FO4 leg of #6089 (closed, FO3 leg, a9472103b) and #5505 (closed, Oblivion leg). No audit has measured FO4. The closed-issue search for `"Use Land Data"` returns only #5505, #6089 and #6407.
- **Description**: A WRLD whose `PNAM` has bit 0x01 set ("Use Land Data", `PNAM_INHERIT_LAND`) takes its landscape from its `WNAM` parent. #5505 and #6089 measured, on Oblivion and FO3 respectively, that such children author no LAND of their own and that their grids sit on top of the parent's terrain. The gate was then widened game by game. FO4 was never measured, so on FO4 `inherit_parent_landscapes` never runs.
- **Evidence**:
  - **Census** (`/tmp/audit/fo4/py/wrld_land.py`). These are the only FO4 WRLDs with the bit set:

    | Worldspace | Plugin | Parent | Cells | Own LAND | LAND-less cells with refs |
    |---|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
    | `Goodneighbor` 0x00054BD5 | Fallout4.esm | Commonwealth 0x3C | 109 | 0 | 14 |
    | `NukaWorldMarket` 0x01053C58 (plugin-local) | DLCNukaWorld.esm | NukaWorld 0x0100290F | 59 | 0 | 8 |
    | `NukaWorldAmphitheater` 0x01052931 | DLCNukaWorld.esm | NukaWorld | 63 | 2 | 7 |

    All three carry `PNAM` 0x001F. DLCCoast, DLCRobot and DLCworkshop03 have none.
  - **Co-location test**, using #6089's method (`/tmp/audit/fo4/py/colocate.py`, `colocate_dlc.py`): compare each child STAT REFR's `DATA` z with the parent's `VHGT` height at the same (x, y). Parent LAND exists at every sampled point.

    | Worldspace | Co-located median \|dz\| | Shifted-control medians |
    |---|---:|---|
    | NukaWorldMarket | **69** | 448 / 1,783 / 2,694 |
    | NukaWorldAmphitheater | **168** | 713 / 1,476 / 1,878 |
    | Goodneighbor | 786 | 624 / 752 / 1,595 |

    - The two Nuka-World results are decisive, at the same strength as FO3's Oasis (166 vs 2,480).
    - Goodneighbor is a dense city, so its median is inconclusive. Even so, 14.2% of its 6,106 statics sit within 48 units of the parent terrain, against 0.8–4.0% for the far controls.
- **Impact**:
  - Entering Nuka-World Market or the Amphitheater, and very likely Goodneighbor, gives 15–29 content cells no terrain mesh and no ground collision.
  - The player and NPCs depend on placed statics, or fall.
  - The parent-keyed distant LOD ring does not cover these cells, because the child authors a CELL at those coordinates.
- **Related**: #6089, #5505, #6407 (one predicate feeds both the step and `exterior_ready`), #6043.
- **Suggested Fix**: Add `Fallout4` to `needs_exterior_processing`. This one gate also covers the shared-parse cache. Add an `--ignored` FO4 census pin over the three worldspaces, for example asserting that the fill count is at least 29. Confirm Goodneighbor visually before relying on it.

#### FO4-2026-10-27-02: `BPTD` `BPND` is decoded with the FO3/FNV layout on FO4's 101-byte payload
- **Severity**: LOW. This is latent: no FO4 consumer reads the parts. The warning noise is the #5493 class, but about 20× smaller.
- **Dimension**: 4. These are FO4 records through the shared ESM decoder. The mechanism belongs to `/audit-esm`.
- **Location**:
  - `crates/plugin/src/esm/records/misc/equipment.rs:245-300`: `parse_bptd`, whose BPND arm (`:274`) reads fixed offsets 0–20 and runs `remap_fid` on `@12` and `@16`.
  - `crates/plugin/src/esm/records/dispatch_misc_gameplay_b.rs:148-157`: the BPTD dispatch arm, which has no game gate.
  - Introduced by aebcc99a1 (Refs #6350), whose layout was "corpus-established against vanilla FalloutNV.esm and Fallout3.esm".
- **Status**: NEW. Related:
  - ESM-2026-10-27-01 covers the FNV/FO3 byte order inside the same 84-byte struct. That is a different defect from this game-layout mismatch.
  - #6350 (open).
- **Evidence**:
  - **BPND sizes per game:**

    | Master | BPND payloads | Size |
    |---|---:|---:|
    | Fallout4.esm (40 BPTD) | 405 | **101 B** |
    | FalloutNV.esm | 304 | 84 B |
    | Fallout3.esm | 207 | 84 B |
    | Skyrim SE (base + 2 DLC) | 108 | 84 B |

    Only FO4 differs.
  - **A decoded FO4 payload.** In Fallout4.esm `DefaultBodyPartData` (0x1D), every part's BPND starts `00 00 80 3f | 00×8 | 00 00 80 3f …`. The FNV layout reads the float 1.0 at `@12` as `generic_debris` FormID 0x3F800000, and reads `part_type`, `to_hit_chance` and `health_percent` as 0.
  - **Warning count.** On the vanilla 7-master load, each DLC's BPTD is parsed with one master. Its 0x3F… values reach `FormIdRemap::remap`'s multi-plugin out-of-range arm (`reader.rs:601-609`, `log::warn!`): DLCRobot 26, DLCCoast 86, DLCNukaWorld 40, for **152 false "FormID 3f800000 has mod_index 63 but plugin has 1 masters" warnings**. Fallout4.esm has no masters, so its 405 values log at debug only.
- **Impact**:
  - Today: no gameplay effect. FO4's `CharacterRulesProfile` has `vats: None`, so `build_vats_model` returns `None`, and `vats_scan` / `resolve_limb_table` never read the table.
  - The 152 warnings bury real remap failures in the log, as #5493's FLTR spam did.
  - The stored `BptdRecord.parts` are wrong data waiting for a future FO4 VATS or dismemberment consumer.
- **Suggested Fix**:
  - Decode BPND fields only for the 84-byte dialect (FO3/FNV/Skyrim), by gating on game or on payload length.
  - On FO4, keep the BPTN/BPNN/BPNT/BPNI name run and leave the payload undecoded until a sourced FO4 layout (xEdit `wbDefinitionsFO4.pas`) is in hand. No guessing.
  - Fold the fix into ESM-2026-10-27-01.
