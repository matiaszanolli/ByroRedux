## Executive Summary

FO4 remains the most complete non-reference game path, and this run confirms it with
full-corpus measurement rather than sampling.

**Confirmed shipped and healthy.** The NIF parser is **100.00% clean over the entire
mesh corpus — 235,082 / 235,082 files (226,009 `.nif` + 9,073 `.bto`/`.btr`), 0 parse
failures, 0 truncations, 0 `NiUnknown` recovery blocks**, with all 115 distinct on-disk
block-type strings dispatching to real parsers. The BA2 reader's exhaustive `{1,2,3,7,8}`
match covers **all 187 installed archives** with zero rejects, and DX10 header
reconstruction produced 14,190 valid DDS buffers across v1/v7/v8 with zero errors and no
unsupported `dxgiFormat`. The BGSM/BGEM parser handles **9,023 material files with zero
failures**. The **M49 CSG precombine pipeline decoded 46,422 of 46,422 shared-geometry
objects (76,498 placed instances) with zero decode errors, zero PSG read errors and zero
unresolved CSG hashes** — the #2369 hash routing, the #1590 owning-plugin path model and
the `887aae52` opaque-architecture alpha-blend guard all verify against real archives.

**The one real gap is distant LOD.** The importer has `synthesize_tangents` but **no
`synthesize_normals`**, and **90.01% of FO4's `.bto`/`.btr` distant-LOD shapes (14,054 of
15,614) ship no authored normals**, so every one of them is handed a constant `[0,1,0]`.
Distant Commonwealth geometry is therefore shaded as if every surface faces straight up.
This is the same defect the Skyrim (20.4%) and Oblivion (100/100 terrain LOD) audits
reported; FO4 is hit hardest on the LOD half and essentially untouched (0.05%) on the
near half.

**Second-order:** the archive chain is CLI-order-first-wins with no load-order alignment,
which silently shadows **1,681 DLC precombine re-bakes** (each of which names a
*different* CSG blob than its base-game namesake) and **the entire 3,851-entry HD texture
pack**.

**Skill drift found and reported.** Three of this skill's own checklist items chase
content FO4 does not ship: the Dimension-4 collision regression pin names two `bhk*`
shape types of which the corpus contains **zero** (FO4 collision is 100% the opaque
`bhkPhysicsSystem` blob); the "Next-Gen patch BSVER range" does not exist (**all 226,009
NIFs are BSVER exactly 130** — Next-Gen changed only the BA2 container revision); and
**MOVS has zero records in every FO4 master** (0 byte-occurrences of the FourCC anywhere),
making both the "FO4 architecture record" listing and the "MOVS physics runtime" forward
scope item dead weight.

**Stale candidates verified and dropped: 8** (see *Verification Ledger*), plus one open
GitHub issue (**#3512**) whose premise the `#3410` fix has already invalidated.

## Findings by Dimension

### HIGH

#### FO4-2026-08-30-D4-01 — 90% of FO4 distant-LOD geometry renders with a fabricated constant `[0,1,0]` normal
*Dimension 4 · labels: `game:fo4`, `legacy-compat`, `nif`, `renderer`*

`crates/nif/src/import/mesh/` provides `synthesize_tangents` and `synthesize_tangents_yup`
but **no normal synthesis**. Every geometry path substitutes a literal
`vec![[0.0, 1.0, 0.0]; positions.len()]` when the source authors no normals —
`ni_tri_shape.rs:139`, `bs_tri_shape.rs:90`, `bs_geometry.rs:249`, `sse_recon.rs:510`.

MEASURED over the complete `.bto`/`.btr` corpus (9,073 files; FO4 sits on the Creation
`.bto`/`.btr` ladder per `cell_loader/lod_bands.rs`, consumed via
`cell_loader/placement_lod.rs`):

| `vertex_desc` attribute mask | shapes | normals | tangents | UVs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `0x0003` VERTEX \| UVS | 8,271 | ✗ | ✗ | ✓ |
| `0x0001` VERTEX only | 5,783 | ✗ | ✗ | ✗ |
| `0x001b` VERTEX \| UVS \| NORMALS \| TANGENTS | 1,560 | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |

**14,054 of 15,614 LOD shapes (90.01%) carry no authored normals.** All 14,054 also lack
tangents; 5,783 also lack UVs. Consequence: sunlit and shadowed faces of the same distant
building shade identically, and no tangent-space normal mapping can run on distant
architecture. The triangle list needed to derive per-face/per-vertex normals **is present**
in every one of these files.

Near-corpus contrast (why this is LOD-specific): only 62 of 130,480 imported `.nif` meshes
(0.05%) hit the same fallback.

Cross-check status: same root cause as the Skyrim / Oblivion reports; this entry records
FO4's measured exposure and the fact that the LOD path is where it bites here.

### MEDIUM

#### FO4-2026-08-30-D1-01 — mesh/texture archive lookup is CLI-order-first-wins, silently shadowing 1,871 DLC overrides and the entire HD texture pack
*Dimension 1 · labels: `game:fo4`, `legacy-compat`, `asset-provider`*

`TextureProvider::extract_mesh` / `::extract`
(`byroredux/src/asset_provider/texture.rs:57` and `:31`) iterate `mesh_archives` /
`texture_archives` and return the **first** hit; `build_texture_provider` appends archives
in raw `--bsa` / `--textures-bsa` CLI order with no load-order alignment, no last-wins
rule, and no shadow diagnostic. Bethesda's own precedence is the opposite: a later archive
overrides an earlier one.

MEASURED:
- **1,681 `_oc.nif` paths exist in BOTH `Fallout4 - MeshesExtra.ba2` and a DLC
  `- Main.ba2`.** All 1,681 differ byte-for-byte, **and all 1,681 name a different
  `BSPackedGeomObject::filename_hash`** (base → `ddf19a67`/`Fallout4 - Geometry.csg`;
  DLC → the DLC's own blob). Whichever archive is listed first therefore decides which
  *bake* renders — silently, because the #2369 hash routing makes both decode cleanly.
- Total DLC mesh entries shadowed when the base archives are listed first: **1,871**
  (1,761 precombines + 110 ordinary meshes) — DLCCoast 633, DLCNukaWorld 559, DLCRobot 427,
  DLCworkshop03 249, DLCworkshop01 3.
- Textures: 4 DLC entries — and **`DLCUltraHighResolution`: 3,851 of 3,851 entries (100%)
  collide with `Fallout4 - Textures*.ba2`**, so the HD pack is entirely inert unless the
  user happens to list it first.

The natural invocation — archives in the same order as `--master`/`--esm` — is exactly the
broken one. Fix shape: reverse-iterate the chain (or insert at the front), and log a
shadow count per archive at open time.

*Dimension 6 · labels: `game:fo4`, `legacy-compat`, `esm`*

`decal_data` has exactly one write (`esm/cell/support.rs:555`) and **no consumer anywhere
in the tree** — `grep -rn decal_data --include='*.rs' .` yields 7 hits: 1 write, 1 field
declaration, 5 test lines. MEASURED: **303 vanilla DODT payloads** across the masters
(Fallout4 207/382, DLCCoast 31/37, DLCNukaWorld 63/73, DLCRobot 1/1, DLCworkshop03 1/1 —

#### FO4-2026-08-30-D2-01 — `smoothness == 1.0` with no gloss map pins roughness at the 0.04 floor
*Dimension 2 · labels: `game:fo4`, `legacy-compat`, `material`*

`asset_provider/material.rs:1358` writes `roughness_override = (1.0 - smoothness).clamp(0.04, 1.0)`;
`triangle.frag:1255` then applies `roughness = mix(1.0, roughness, glossTexel.r)` — but only
when `mat.glossMapIndex != 0u`. MEASURED: **6,203 of 8,330 BGSMs (74.5%) author
`smoothness == 1.0`** (the Bethesda Material Editor default — `MaterialLib/BGSM.cs:32`,
tooltip "Smoothness of the specular effect", with per-texel data in the `SmoothSpec` slot).
Of those, **345 author no leaf `smooth_spec_texture` and 80 more name a DDS absent from all
15 texture archives → ≤425 materials (5.1%) end up at roughness 0.04 with nothing to
modulate them** — near-mirror dielectrics, concentrated on hair, eyeballs and creature glow.
Upper bound: a template parent can still supply the slot via `resolved.walk()`. The
translation mirrors the source authoring; the gap is the absence of a neutral fallback when
the modulating map is unavailable.

#### FO4-2026-08-30-D4-02 — APP_CULLED geometry with a live visibility controller is dropped at import
*Dimension 4 · labels: `game:fo4`, `legacy-compat`, `nif`*

`import/walk/mod.rs:441` (and three sibling sites) `return` on `av.flags & 0x01`.
MEASURED: 581 of 136,948 non-`_oc` BSTriShapes (0.42%) are APP_CULLED; 14 files have
**all** shapes culled and import to zero geometry, and **13 of those 14 also ship a
`NiVisController` / `NiBoolTimelineInterpolator` / `BSNiAlphaPropertyTestRefController`**
whose entire purpose is to un-hide them. Because the shape never reaches the ECS, no
controller can ever make it visible. Correct shape: import culled shapes with
`visible = false` when a visibility channel targets them.

#### FO4-2026-08-30-D1-02 — precombine LOD tie-break relies on `max_by_key`'s last-wins rather than a stated rule
*Dimension 1 · labels: `game:fo4`, `legacy-compat`*

`build_precombine_meshes` selects `(0..3).max_by_key(|&(c, _)| c)`. Rust's `max_by_key`
returns the **last** maximum, so on a tie the highest LOD index wins by accident.
MEASURED: **49 of 46,422 objects (0.11%)** have two or more LODs sharing the maximum
triangle count. Visually equivalent (alternative triangulations of one surface), so this is
a determinism/intent nit, not a rendering defect — but the tie-break deserves a stated rule.
For context, the selection itself is doing real work: LOD0 wins 28,494 times, LOD1 7,012,
LOD2 10,916 — 38.6% of objects have their finest triangulation at index 1 or 2.

#### FO4-2026-08-30-D4-03 (SKILL DRIFT) — the Dimension-4 collision regression pin names two block types FO4 ships zero of
*Dimension 4 · labels: `game:fo4`, `documentation`*

The checklist calls `BhkMultiSphereShape` and `BhkConvexListShape` an "FO4 collision
regression pin … power-armor / settlement / destructible-debris collision depends on this".
MEASURED over the full 226,009-file header census, **the only `bhk*` type strings present
anywhere in FO4 are** `bhkNPCollisionObject` (86,443 files), `bhkPhysicsSystem` (86,291) and
`bhkRagdollSystem` (188). Zero occurrences of `bhkMultiSphereShape`, `bhkConvexListShape`,
`bhkRigidBody`, `bhkBoxShape`, `bhkSphereShape`, `bhkCapsuleShape`, `bhkConvexVerticesShape`,
`bhkMoppBvTreeShape`, `bhkPackedNiTriStripsShape`, `bhkListShape`, `bhkTransformShape`,
`bhkCompressedMeshShape`, or any `bhkConstraint`. FO4 collision is 100% the opaque
Havok-serialised `bhkPhysicsSystem` blob. Both `resolve_shape` arms are correct and present
(`import/collision/shape.rs:110`, `:235`) and must stay for Oblivion/FO3/FNV/Skyrim — but
the FO4 skill should stop nominating them.

#### FO4-2026-08-30-D4-04 (SKILL/DOC DRIFT) — there is no "Next-Gen patch BSVER"; all FO4 NIFs are BSVER 130
*Dimension 4 · labels: `game:fo4`, `documentation`*

The skill and its game-context table describe the NIF band as "BSVER 130 + Next-Gen patch
range … `FALLOUT4..FO4_DLC_UPPER` (130..=139)". MEASURED: **every one of the 226,009 NIFs
in this Next-Gen-patched installation is BSVER exactly 130, and every one is NIF version
20.2.0.7.** Nothing in 131..=139 ships. The Next-Gen update revised the **BA2 container**
(v7 textures / v8 meshes — 90 of the 187 installed archives) and left the NIF BSVER alone.
The 130..=139 band, the DLC trailing-field gate and the `FO4_SHADER_GAP` (131) handling are
forward-compat only, exercised by no vanilla content. Worth stating so future auditors do
not hunt for 132–139 test cases.

#### FO4-2026-08-30-D6-03 (SKILL/ROADMAP DRIFT) — FO4 ships zero MOVS records
*Dimension 6 · labels: `game:fo4`, `documentation`, `esm`*

Verified two ways: a full GRUP walk of all 7 masters returns **MOVS = 0**, and the 4-byte
sequence `MOVS` appears **0 times** anywhere in `Fallout4.esm`, `DLCCoast.esm`,
`DLCNukaWorld.esm` or `DLCRobot.esm`. `crates/plugin/src/esm/records/movs.rs` +
`parse_movs_group` are gated on `is_fo4_plus` and warn "MOVS is FO4+ only" for other games,
but no FO4 master exercises the arm. The skill lists MOVS as one of four "FO4 architecture
records" and carries "MOVS physics runtime" in its forward scope; both target a record type
with zero vanilla instances. Demote both to "modded content only".

#### FO4-2026-08-30-D9-01 — the "100.00% / 235,082" headline conflates the `.nif` and LOD halves
*Dimension 9 · labels: `game:fo4`, `documentation`*

ROADMAP's `100.00% (235,082 / 235,082)` is correct as a **parse** statistic and was
re-measured exactly this run. But 9,073 of those files are `.bto`/`.btr` distant-LOD meshes
in which 90.01% of shapes get fabricated normals (D4-01). "100% clean" is being read as
"renders correctly". Recommend the compat-matrix row split the `.nif` (226,009) and LOD
(9,073) halves, or footnote the LOD normal gap, so the headline does not vouch for geometry
whose attributes the importer is inventing.

  **176,569 (78.1%) are precombine `_oc.nif`** — all import to zero meshes on the standard

  DLCCoast (14,247), DLCRobot (5,290), DLCworkshop03 (2,956), DLCworkshop01 (18).

  its own cells under `<plugin>.esm\` and its base-game re-bakes at root (2,996 files).
