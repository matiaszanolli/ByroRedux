# FO3 Compatibility Audit — 2026-10-05

**HEAD**: `a2c24b16e` · **Baseline**: `docs/audits/AUDIT_FO3_2026-10-03.md` (HEAD `be3cd9468`) ·
**Audited**: Dimensions 1, 2, 3, 4, 5. Every dimension had commits on its paths since the baseline: 3 / 9 / 17 / 3 / 8. ·
**Unchanged since baseline (skimmed)**: none. Dims 1 and 4 had doc-only or test-only production deltas and got guard-level checks.

**Scope**: where Fallout 3 diverges from the FNV path. FO3 shares FNV's NIF v20.2.0.7 / BSVER 34, BSA v104, ESM parser,
cell loader and RT pipeline (`GameKind::Fallout3NV`; only `CharacterRulesProfile` tells them apart).

**Method** (part of `/audit-suite --preset comprehensive`):
- All five dimensions ran synchronously in one agent, with no sub-agents.
- Every real-data figure was measured at HEAD against `Fallout 3 goty/Data/` (base + 5 DLC).
- Measurement tools:
  - the committed `--ignored` real-data tests, with plugin tests run one at a time with `--exact`;
  - a throwaway probe crate in the session scratchpad (`fo3probe`, read-only use of `byroredux-plugin`), which parses `Fallout3.esm` plus the 5 DLC masters with a load-order remap;
  - `byroredux` bin tests on toolchain 1.96.0.
- **No engine, Vulkan process or smoke script was run.** GPU entity counts, FPS, hand rendering, the seam blend and the live P0/P2/P5 gates are *not measured*.
- **Dedup sources**:
  - the cached open-issue list (`/tmp/audit/issues.json`, 97 open);
  - all 24 `docs/audits/*_2026-10-05.md` reports, including the concurrent `AUDIT_FNV_2026-10-05.md`.
- Some `--ignored` bin results come from the FNV audit's log (`/tmp/audit/fnv/t_bin_ignored.log`, same HEAD). They are cited as such rather than re-run.
