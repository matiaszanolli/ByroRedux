### FO3-SD-01: Model-less templated `CREA` shells spawn nothing: 2,641 of 4,397 placed FO3 creature references (60.1%)
- **Severity**: HIGH
- **Dimension**: 5 (ACRE creature spawn)
- **Location**: `byroredux/src/npc_spawn/resumable/runtime.rs:445-460` (`prepare_creature_state` derives skeleton and NIFZ from the **shell's** `npc.model_path`), `:543-546` (`if state.skeleton_path.is_empty() { return UnitOutcome::Complete(None); }`), `byroredux/src/cell_loader/references/mod.rs:756` (`if let Some(root) = result.root` skips every identity / script / count stamp), `crates/plugin/src/equip.rs:688-` (`ResolvedNpc` has no Use Model/Animation terminal).
- **Status**: Existing (suite): **FNV-SD-01** (`AUDIT_FNV_2026-10-19.md`, same code, same mechanism); #4137 (open) records the bit as "no consumer". FO3 leg quantified here.
- **Description**: FO3 spawns its leveled fauna and super mutants as `CREA` shells with no `MODL` whose `TPLT` points at a `LVLC` and whose ACBS template flags carry `0x0040` Use Model/Animation. The loader resolves every other template category but not this one, so the shell's empty model path yields an empty skeleton path and the Skeleton phase returns `Complete(None)`. The placement root made by `spawn_placement_root` (with `ActorValues`, `FactionRanks`, `CreatureAttack`, `Transform`) stays in the world as an invisible, body-less actor.
- **Evidence** (raw-byte census of `Fallout3.esm` + Anchorage, The Pitt, Broken Steel, Point Lookout, Zeta, FormIDs remapped to load order, last writer wins; independent of the Rust parser):
  - 894 `CREA` records: 206 have no `MODL`; 181 carry `TPLT` with template bit `0x0040` (174 point at a `LVLC`); the other 25 are `AudioTemplate*` markers that are never placed.
  - Placed references (`ACRE` + `ACHR`) naming a `CREA`: **4,397**; naming a model-less shell: **2,641 (60.1%)**, 154 distinct shells (`Lvl*`, `*LvlSupermutant*`, `LvlRobot*`, `LvlTurret*`). In `Fallout3.esm` alone: 2,152 of 3,433.
  - Every one resolves through its `LVLC` to a modelled terminal (`Creatures\SMSpineBreaker\Skeleton.nif`, `Protectron`, `MisterGutsy`, `SentryTurret` ...), so a Use-Model terminal would serve all of them.
  - The modelled bases' 1,756 placements do spawn; all 1,263 of their `NIFZ` entries resolve in the archives.
- **Disproof attempted**: (1) a parse-time template merge fills `model_path`: `dispatch_actor.rs` CREA arm only sets `is_creature`, clears `class_form_id`, ORs `starts_dead`; (2) a second spawn path handles shells: `record_index.actor()` returns the shell and `NpcSpawnJob::runtime` is the only creature route; (3) the shells are markers: the 25 marker shells (`marker_creature.nif`) are a different set. Not verified live (no engine).
- **Impact**: 60% of FO3 creature placements (radroaches, mole rats, super mutants, Talon / Enclave robots, turrets, ants) are absent in exterior and interior cells. Fix and the animation fix (FO3-SD-05) belong together.
- **Related**: FNV-SD-01, FO3-SD-05, #4137, #2567, #5495.
- **Suggested Fix**: as FNV-SD-01: add a `Use Model/Animation` terminal to `ResolvedNpc` and derive skeleton / NIFZ / clips from it; despawn or stamp the orphan root on `Complete(None)`.

### FO3-SD-03: FO3 child worldspaces with "Use Land Data" draw no ground; #5505's landscape inheritance is Oblivion-only
- **Severity**: MEDIUM
- **Dimension**: 3 (worldspace context, terrain)
- **Location**: `byroredux/src/cell_loader/exterior.rs:1795-1805` (`if record_index.game == GameKind::Oblivion { inherit_parent_landscapes() }`), `crates/plugin/src/esm/cell/index.rs:358-425` (the function, whose doc says "FO3+ child grids are not assumed to share the parent's coordinates").
- **Status**: **NEW**. Related: #5505 (closed; Oblivion leg). It never claimed FO3 and records the co-location as "measured on Oblivion only". This run measures it on FO3.
- **Description**: WRLD `PNAM` bit `0x01` ("Use Land Data") makes a child worldspace use its `WNAM` parent's landscape. Four FO3 worldspaces carry it: `Oasis` (`0x0005`), `ParadiseFalls` (`0x003d`), `GNRroofWorld` (`0x0007`), `MegatonWorld` (`0xffc5`), all with parent `Wasteland`. Their cells are authored without LAND. For FO3 the loader never fills them, so those cells spawn their references with no terrain mesh and no ground collision.
- **Evidence** (raw scan of `Fallout3.esm`; `LAND` attributed to the preceding `CELL`):

  | Worldspace | cells | LAND-less | of those with refs | parent LAND at same (x,y) |
  |---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
  | Oasis | 34 | **34** | 7 | 34 |
  | ParadiseFalls | 13 | 11 | 10 | 11 |
  | GNRroofWorld | 143 | 58 | 4 | 58 |
  | MegatonWorld | 16 | 5 | 1 | 5 |
  | total | 206 | 108 | 22 | 108 |

  - The co-location test the #5505 author asked for: static objects of the child cells against the parent's `VHGT` terrain height at the same position. `Oasis`: median |dz| **166** units, against **2,480** for a control shifted by (+7,+5) cells. `ParadiseFalls`: **46** against **4,126**. So both towns stand on the Wasteland terrain at those coordinates. `GNRroofWorld` (1,547 vs 936) and `MegatonWorld` (431 vs 717) are inconclusive (rooftop and offset geometry), not disproved.
- **Disproof attempted**: (1) another source of terrain for child cells: the LOD ring is parent-keyed only for coordinates the child has no CELL for, and `Oasis` has `PNAM` without the LOD bit; (2) the cells are hidden-quad cells: `XCLC` land flags are not what is missing (#6043 is about Hide Quad flags); (3) the bit means something else on FO3: the `GNRroofWorld`/`ParadiseFalls`/`Oasis` values `0x07`/`0x3d`/`0x05` have bit 0 set and the parent chain exists; xEdit's FO3 `PNAM` list is the same as TES5's. Not verified live.
- **Impact**: entering Paradise Falls or the Oasis puts the player in a world whose ground is missing (22 cells with placed content have none).
- **Related**: #5505, `EsmCellIndex::inherit_parent_landscapes`, EXT-D6 sibling (terrain LOD hole).
- **Suggested Fix**: run the inheritance for `Fallout3NV` too (the CS rule is the same), keep the cell's own LAND winning, and pin it with an `--ignored` test over `Oasis` / `ParadiseFalls`; confirm the roof and Megaton worlds visually before relying on them.

### FO3-SD-04: The RACE body-section `ICON` skin is never applied on FO3
- **Severity**: MEDIUM
- **Dimension**: 5 (NPC body assembly)
- **Location**: `byroredux/src/npc_spawn.rs:689-702` (`humanoid_body_path_icon_index` returns `None` for every game but Oblivion), `byroredux/src/npc_spawn/resumable/runtime.rs:249-254` and `:131-147` (`race_body_texture` is only reached through it), `crates/plugin/src/esm/records/actor/race.rs:85-94` (doc: "FO3 / FNV author body textures in the head-section table instead; this list is empty there").
- **Status**: **NEW**. Related: #5487 (closed; Oblivion only), #5783.
- **Description**: The Oblivion arm of #5487 paints the RACE body-section `ICON` over the body NIF's embedded skin. FO3 authors the same data and the parser already collects it (`body_part_textures`), but the index mapping exists only for Oblivion, so every FO3 body piece keeps the skin texture baked into the NIF. The doc comment on `body_part_textures` states the opposite of the data.
- **Evidence**:
  - `Fallout3.esm` RACE records carry `ICON` under `NAM1` (the body section): `Ghoul` → `Characters\Ghoul\UpperBodyMale.dds`, `Characters\Ghoul\HandMale.dds`; the four `*Raider` races → `Characters\Raider\UpperBodyMale.dds`, `HandMale.dds` (female: `RaiderFemale`); the four `*Child` races → `Characters\Child\UpperBodyMale.dds`, `Characters\ChildFemale\...`. All 12 textures exist in `Fallout - Textures.bsa`. FO3 body indices are 0 upper body, 1 left hand, 2 right hand (not Oblivion's 0 / 1 leg / 2 hand / 3 foot).
  - The body NIFs embed the default skin: `upperbody.nif` → `characters\male\UpperBodyMale.dds`; `lefthand.nif` → `characters\male\HandMale.dds`; **`childupperbody.nif` → `characters\male\UpperBodyMale.dds` (adult male)**; `femaleupperbody.nif` → `characters\female\UpperBodyFemale.dds`.
  - Placed humanoids whose resolved race has a different body ICON: raiders 417 of 3,252 ACHR, ghouls 122, children 62: **~600 (18%)**. Raiders are FO3's most common hostile. FNV's RACE records author identical ICONs for Raider, Ghoul and Child, so the FNV path is hit the same way (not separately censused here).
  - The head already gets its race ICON (`head_part_texture`, `runtime.rs:294-295`); the ghoul teeth / tongue ICONs (`TeethLowerGhoul.dds` ...) are unused too.
- **Disproof attempted**: (1) the armor covers the body: most raiders wear armor, so the exposed skin is the hands, neck and head; the naked-body case still occurs for the child, ghoul and underwear NPCs; (2) a second mechanism recolours the skin: `grep` for `body_textures` shows only the Oblivion fill (`runtime.rs:249-254`) and the Body phase consumer (`:590-600`); (3) the INDX scheme does not line up: it does not, which is why a plain reuse of the Oblivion table would be wrong. Not verified live.
- **Impact**: ghoul NPCs have human bodies under ghoul heads, children wear adult male skin, raiders have default instead of tanned skin.
- **Related**: #5487, #5783, FO3-SD-10 (the doc).
- **Suggested Fix**: add a FO3/FNV mapping (0 upper body, 1 left hand, 2 right hand) in `humanoid_body_path_icon_index`; `upperbody.nif` also carries underwear and gore-cap meshes, so apply the texture to the skin mesh only (the Body phase currently sets `base_color` on every mesh of the NIF).

### FO3-SD-06: The runtime-FaceGen recipe and gender come from the shell, not the Use-Traits terminal
- **Severity**: MEDIUM
- **Dimension**: 5 (NPC spawn, templated actors)
- **Location**: `byroredux/src/npc_spawn/resumable/runtime.rs:222` (gender from `npc.acbs_flags`), `:306` (hair, eyebrow, eyes, eye / mouth / ear part selection from `npc.runtime_facegen`), `:982` (FGGS / FGGA head morphs from the same recipe); race is correctly the template's (`references/mod.rs:718`).
