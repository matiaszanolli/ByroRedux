### Dim 2 — ESM slice

#### FO3-2026-10-21-01: The LTEX Havok shipped-master census skips Fallout 3
- **Severity**: LOW
- **Dimension**: 2 (ESM slice)
- **Location**: `crates/plugin/tests/parse_real_esm.rs:1314-1349` (`ltex_havok_data_decodes_on_shipped_masters`)
- **Status**: NEW (test gap). The FO3 skill names this gap ("FO3's 51 LTEX material bytes are unmeasured").
- **Description**: #6048's real-data census covers Oblivion (218), FNV (89) and Skyrim (68). FO3 decodes through the same pre-Skyrim `HNAM` arm, but no test pins its count or that its material bytes stay inside the FO3 Havok enum.
- **Evidence**: raw-byte census of `Fallout3.esm` (independent of the Rust parser):
  - All 51 LTEX carry a 3-byte `HNAM`.
  - Material bytes: 0 Stone ×7, 2 Dirt ×26, 4 Grass ×6, 10 Heavy Stone ×1, 18 Sand ×3, 19 Broken Concrete ×8.
  - Friction and restitution are 30/30 on all 51.
  - Examples: `LDirtWasteland01` (`0x15457`) = `02 1e 1e`; `LAsphaltWasteland01` = `13 1e 1e`; `GravelRoadWasteland01` = `12 1e 1e`.
- **Impact**: the FO3 leg of the decode is unguarded, so an FO3-only regression in the shared arm passes CI and the existing census.
- **Related**: #6048, `c53d87679`.
- **Suggested Fix**: add `(FO3_ENV, FO3_DEFAULT, "Fallout3.esm", Some(31), 51)` to the census table, and drop the FO3 caveat from the skill.

#### FO3-2026-10-21-02: Default-land tiles (92 % of FO3 LAND) get the static default material (0, Stone)
- **Severity**: LOW
- **Dimension**: 2 / 3 (terrain collider material)
- **Location**: `byroredux/src/cell_loader/terrain.rs:921-967` (`dominant_land_texture` returns `None` when LTEX 0, the default land, dominates; `land_surface_material` then yields `None`, and the collider keeps the static default 0). `DefaultLandTexture::for_game` (`terrain.rs:836-865`) renders those quadrants with `textures\landscape\dirtwasteland01.dds`.
- **Status**: Existing: **#6048** (open; residual of `c53d87679`, which is "part of #6048"). FO3 leg quantified here.
- **Description**: the renderer paints a BTXT-less quadrant with the executable's default land texture. The collider path treats the same quadrant as "no material" and falls back to `surface_material` 0, which the footstep probe and impact audio read as STONE. On FO3, the texture the engine draws there is the one FO3's own `LDirtWasteland01` LTEX classifies as Dirt (2). The two answers disagree on almost all of FO3's terrain.
- **Evidence**: raw census of every `LAND` in `Fallout3.esm`, reproducing `dominant_land_texture`'s layer composite:
  - 148,211 of 161,024 quadrants (92.0 %) author no `BTXT`.
  - **37,113 of 40,256 tiles (92.2 %)** are default-dominant and so carry material 0.
  - FNV, for comparison: 25,401 of 29,362 (86.5 %). The mechanism is shared.
- **Impact**: latent today. `FootstepConfig.surface_sounds` is empty until IPDS decoding lands, so every footstep takes the default sound (`byroredux/src/systems/audio.rs:322-326`). Once per-surface sounds land, about 92 % of the Capital Wasteland walks as stone. #6048's gain therefore reaches only about 8 % of FO3 tiles.
- **Related**: #6048, FO3-2026-10-21-01.
- **Suggested Fix**: source the material the executable assigns to the default land before mapping anything (no-guess policy). Do not assume it is the matching LTEX's `HNAM`. If no source exists, record the gap beside `DefaultLandTexture` so the "None means default" contract is explicit.
