## Executive Summary

FNV's foundations hold. Every FNV real-data lane is green: the ESM parse (78,575 records, equal to the ROADMAP figure), the NIF parse-rate / per-block / unknown-ceiling / torch-emitter lanes, zero constraint drift, both FNV ragdoll imports, 14 FNV real-data `--ignored` tests in the bin crate, and the bin crate's full default suite (3,135 passed; the 10-18 save-shape guard failure, #6050, is fixed). All six 10-19 area findings (#6075–#6079) and nine of the eleven 10-18 findings are closed. Their fixes read correctly, and their real-data pins pass.

The new findings are all **census-against-code** results. The engine now declines things fail-closed, which is the right policy, but on FNV that policy now meets a condition catalog that is narrower than the engine's own state:

- **A quarter of FNV dialogue cannot be selected.** 5,968 of 23,247 INFOs carry a condition function outside the CTDA catalog. 21 quests (2,269 INFOs) are declined whole by their quest-level gate, including all 916 lines of `VNPCFollowers`, the shared companion command set. 284 quest-objective targets are declined too. 2,305 of those INFOs and 198 of those targets are blocked **only** by functions that the M47.3 ObScript VM already answers as script getters, such as `GetItemCount`, `GetObjectiveCompleted`, `GetDisabled` and `IsInCombat`. The engine has two condition evaluators, and the one that gates dialogue lags the one that runs scripts (FNV-2026-10-21-01).
- **FNV corpses ignore their authored pose.** 276 dead-base `ACHR` placements carry an 18-entry `XRGD` pose that is dropped at two layers. The placement flag that parks the pose is TES5+-only, and the pose applier accepts only Skyrim/FO4 rigs (FNV-2026-10-21-02).
- **The closed-HIGH pin runner is red on a clean tree.** It reports three FNV pins that passed as "green no-op" failures, because it counts split `--nocapture` lines (FNV-2026-10-21-03).

**0 CRITICAL · 0 HIGH · 4 MEDIUM · 2 LOW.** The 6 findings are 4 NEW and 2 Existing with new FNV evidence (#6090, #5495). Two more issues are carried and not counted (#6053, #4232).

## Findings

### MEDIUM

#### FNV-2026-10-21-01: A quarter of FNV dialogue and objective targets are declined fail-closed because the CTDA catalog lags the ObScript VM's own getters (and lacks `GetIsVoiceType`)
- **Severity**: MEDIUM
- **Dimension**: 5 (dialogue / quests through the M47.1 condition mechanism). **Owner of the mechanism**: `/audit-scripting` (catalog), `/audit-gameplay` Dim 2 (dialogue selection).
- **Location**: `crates/scripting/src/condition/catalog.rs:306` (`ROWS`, 53 indices) and `:396` (`FOREIGN_INDICES`); `crates/scripting/src/condition/eval.rs:87` (`is_modeled`); the fail-closed consumers are `DialogueQuestConditions` (#5734), INFO selection (#5752) and the objective-target / log-entry gates (#5993, `9b6dcf872`). The getters that already exist: `crates/scripting/src/obscript_effects.rs:1095-1136` (`CMD_GET_ITEM_COUNT` `0x102f`, `CMD_IS_IN_COMBAT` `0x1121`, `CMD_GET_HEALTH_PERCENTAGE` `0x11af`, `CMD_GET_DEAD_COUNT` `0x1054`), `crates/scripting/src/obscript_quests.rs:76-79` (`GetObjectiveCompleted` `0x11a4`, `GetObjectiveDisplayed` `0x11a5`, `GetQuestCompleted` `0x1222`), `crates/scripting/src/obscript_actions.rs:54/136` (`GetDisabled` `0x1023`, through `reference_enabled`), `crates/scripting/src/obscript_more.rs:346/372` (`GetIsCreature` `0x1040`, `GetPCIsSex` `0x1083`).
- **Status**: NEW. Related: #5750 (open; Skyrim's `GetIsVoiceType` 426 on the Hello topic; it does not cover FNV's 427 or the quest-level gate), #5752 / #5734 / #5993 (closed; they established the fail-closed policy, which is correct and should stay), CHAR-2026-10-21-M-02 (once 427 is catalogued it inherits that finding's Use-Traits `VTCK` read).
- **Description**: The fail-closed gates decline any INFO, quest or objective target whose conditions name a function `is_modeled` rejects. The ObScript getter id of a function is `0x1000 + CTDA index` (`evaluate_script_getter`'s own doc), and M47.3 implemented many of those getters natively. None of them was added to the CTDA catalog. The engine therefore keeps the state (inventory, objectives, quest completion, enable state, combat, kill ledger), but the dialogue and quest-target gates cannot read it. `GetIsVoiceType` (FNV 427) is uncatalogued as well. The #5734 commit named cataloguing it as "the follow-up", and no issue tracks that follow-up for FO3/FNV.
- **Evidence** (`/tmp/audit/fnv/probe/qgate.py` and siblings over `FalloutNV.esm`, using the catalog's 53 indices minus FNV's foreign 365/403/550/555; xEdit names from `wbDefinitionsFNV.pas`):
  - Quest-level gates: 93 of 436 quests author one. **21 are declined whole**: 427 `GetIsVoiceType` ×18, 365 `IsChild` ×2 (foreign on FNV), 382 `GetHasNote`, 50 `GetTalkedToPC`, 372 `IsInList`. That removes **2,269 of 23,247 INFOs and 154 of 5,300 `GREETING` INFOs**. Largest: `VNPCFollowers` (916 INFOs, the shared companion commands), `GenericRobot` (357), `vDialogueNCRMilitary` (137, 82 greetings), `vDialogueSuperMutant` (96), `vDialogueSuperMutantFirstGen` (86), `GenericAdultCombat` (71), `GenericKids` (69), `Doctors` (43), `Generic` (28), `GenericAdult` (23), `Followers` (14, fn 372).
  - INFO-level: **5,968 of 23,247 INFOs (25.7%)** carry at least one uncatalogued function: 427 in 2,880, `GetItemCount` 47 in 861, `GetObjectiveCompleted` / `Displayed` 420 / 421 in 683 / 613, `GetScriptVariable` 53 in 333, `GetQuestCompleted` 546 in 315, `GetDeadCount` 84 in 246, `GetPCIsSex` 131 in 176, `IsInCombat` 289 in 125, `GetHealthPercentage` 431 in 84, `GetIsCreature` 64 in 53.
  - **2,305 INFOs are declined only by functions the ObScript VM already answers** ({47, 64, 84, 131, 289, 420, 421, 431, 546}). Another 2,563 are declined only by 427.
  - Objective targets (`QSTA`): 772 of 1,469 are conditioned and **284 are declined**: `GetDisabled` 35 ×94, `GetDeadCount` ×92, `PlayerInRegion` 612 ×52, `GetItemCount` ×37, `GetInCellParam` 230 ×18, `GetObjectiveCompleted` ×18. **198 of them only by implemented getters** (35 included). Log entries: 7 of the 20 conditioned ones are declined.
- **Impact**: Companions cannot offer their shared follower commands. Generic robot, NCR military, super mutant and doctor speakers lose their topic sets, and the selection falls through to whatever lower-priority line passes. Objective markers whose target condition reads `GetDisabled` / `GetDeadCount` never highlight. Nothing is crashed or corrupted, and the policy is the right one. The coverage gap is the bug. The dt1 gate does not see it, because Doc Mitchell's `GREETING` is not in a declined quest.
- **Suggested Fix**: Do not write a second copy. Add catalog rows that call the existing getter bodies, or move those bodies into the evaluator and have the ObScript getters call `evaluate_script_getter`, so that one implementation serves both paths. Catalogue 427 against the speaker's resolved `VTCK` (after CHAR-2026-10-21-M-02's terminal fix) and 372 `IsInList` against `FLST`. Add an FNV real-data floor on the number of declined INFOs and quests, so a later narrowing shows up.

#### FNV-2026-10-21-02: FO3/FNV authored corpse poses (`XRGD`) are dropped at two layers; 276 dead-base FNV `ACHR` placements collapse from their idle pose
- **Severity**: MEDIUM
- **Dimension**: 2 / 4 (ESM slice → spawn → ragdoll). **Owner of the mechanism**: `/audit-gameplay` (corpse spawn), `/audit-physics` (ragdoll).
- **Location**: `crates/plugin/src/esm/cell/walkers.rs:990-992` (`starts_dead` is set only for `Tes5Plus` `ACHR`); `byroredux/src/cell_loader/references/mod.rs:775-782` (the pose is parked only when `placed_ref.starts_dead`, so the FO3/FNV `npc.starts_dead` route never parks it); `byroredux/src/ragdoll_pose.rs:602-620` (`pose_skeleton` accepts only `is_skyrim_posable_ragdoll` / `is_fo4_posable_ragdoll`).
- **Status**: NEW. The 10-19 report (FNV-SD-05) filed it under #5015. #5015 has since closed on its Skyrim/FO4 scope (`18b3d87a7`, `e1bd40bcf`, `dfbcf7e2c`), and the skill text says FO3/FNV `XRGD` posing has no tracking issue. Related: #6065 (FO4 creature rigs), #5005 (the FO3/FNV corpse marker is base health, so `XRGD` is pose only).
- **Description**: On FO3/FNV a corpse is recognised from the base record (`DATA` health ≤ 0 → `NpcRecord::starts_dead`), never from the placement flag. The `XRGD` stamp in `load_references_budgeted` is keyed on the placement flag, so `AuthoredRagdollPose` is never inserted for an FNV corpse. Even if it were, `pose_skeleton` would refuse the FNV humanoid (`Bip01 …`) body set, because no FO3/FNV entry order has been established.
- **Evidence**: census over `FalloutNV.esm` (`/tmp/audit/fnv/probe`, 28-byte entries, base health from `NPC_ DATA` offset 0): **278 `ACHR` carry an 18-entry `XRGD`, 276 of them on a base with health ≤ 0** (the other 2 are posed live actors, which must not be stamped). About 107 `ACRE` carry `XRGD` with 10–37 entries, nearly all on dead bases. The 18 matches the 18-body FNV humanoid ragdoll (`fnv_humanoid_skeleton_threads_ragdoll`, green).
- **Impact**: Every authored FNV corpse (Legion crucifixions, staged massacre scenes, quest bodies) ragdoll-collapses from the idle pose at its placement, instead of lying where the level designer posed it. FO3 shares both layers; it is not measured here.
- **Suggested Fix**: Park the pose on the FO3/FNV route too, gated on `npc.starts_dead && !ragdoll_pose.is_empty()`. Establish the FO3/FNV humanoid entry order the way #6068 did for Skyrim: compare the median per-bone offsets of the 276 poses against `skeleton.nif`'s `bhkRigidBody` order. Then add an `is_fallout_posable_ragdoll` arm. Leave creatures refused until each rig is verified.

### LOW

#### FNV-2026-10-21-03: `scripts/run-real-data-pins.sh` reports three passing FNV pins as "green no-op" failures because it counts `test … ok` lines that `--nocapture` splits
- **Severity**: LOW
- **Dimension**: 6. **Owner**: `/audit-tooling` (audit / CI infrastructure).
- **Location**: `scripts/run-real-data-pins.sh` (the `ran_ok=$(grep -cE '^test .* \.\.\. ok$' …)` check after the `cargo test … --nocapture` call; introduced by `9ee74b6e7`, #6183).
- **Status**: NEW. Related: #6182 (open; no runner registered, so this local script is the lane that actually runs).
- **Description**: A pin whose test prints anything writes its output between `test NAME ... ` and `ok`, so the line never matches `^test .* \.\.\. ok$`. The runner then declares the pin a green no-op and exits 1. `.github/workflows/real-data-gates.yml:225-228` already avoids this for its own jobs ("under --nocapture a test's own output can split its `test ... ok` line") by summing `test result: ok. N passed`. The pin runner duplicates the check without that fix.
- **Evidence**: `scripts/run-real-data-pins.sh fnv` → `ERROR: closed-HIGH pin '…' matched nothing (green no-op)` for `parse_real_fnv_dial_infos_populated`, `fnv_armo_female_meshes_are_parsed_and_selected` and `fnv_leveled_item_multi_pick_semantics_are_pinned_on_the_shipped_master`, each with `test result: ok. 1 passed`. Example from `target/real-data-gates/pins-fnv_armo_female_meshes_are_parsed_and_selected.log` lines 5–6: `test fnv_armo_female_meshes_are_parsed_and_selected ... [#3416] FNV ARMO: 389 total, …` / `ok`. The other 13 FNV pins pass.
- **Impact**: The documented "run every closed-HIGH pin" command can never print "all pins green" on a clean tree. A real failure is therefore hidden among the false ones. Pins for other games that print are presumably hit the same way (not measured).
- **Suggested Fix**: Count with the workflow's expression (`grep -oE 'test result: ok\. [0-9]+ passed'`, summed), ideally from one shared helper that both the workflow and the script call.
