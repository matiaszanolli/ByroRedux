### ESM-2026-10-31-01: FO4 `EXPL` `DATA` is decoded at the form-version-97 layout for every record
- **Severity**: LOW
- **Dimension**: Sub-Record Byte Accounting
- **Record / Sub-record**: `EXPL` / `DATA` (FO4)
- **Location**: `crates/plugin/src/esm/records/misc/effects.rs:447-458` (`parse_expl`, the `Skyrim | Fallout4` arm)
- **Status**: NEW (the arm landed in `c36ab4125`)
- **Description**:
  - The arm reads `Fallout4` as: damage @28, Inner Radius @32, Outer Radius @36.
  - The older xEdit in `/mnt/data/tmp/wbDefinitionsFO4.pas` gates the FO4 field with `wbFromVersion(97, wbFloat('Inner Radius'))`. The current xEdit `dev` branch drops that gate and gates only `Vertical Offset Mult`, by form version 99.
  - The data agrees with the version gate. Of 238 `Fallout4.esm` EXPL, 18 have a `DATA` shorter than the 84-byte full form. Fifteen of them are below form version 97 and carry no Inner Radius, so Outer Radius sits at @32.
- **Evidence** (floats at @24..@44):

  | Record | Form version | `DATA` size | Floats @24..@44 |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | `FakeForce300Explosion` | 63 | 52 | 300, 0, **512**, 0, 0.1 |
  | `FakeForce200Explosion` | 88 | 56 | 200, 0, **128**, 0, 0.1 |
  | `CarNukeTEST` | 95 | 60 | 20000, 200, **550**, 10000, 0.3 |
  | `MirelurkHunterSpitExplosion` | 99 | 64 | 0, 0, 0, **200**, 0 |

  - The 512, 128 and 550 values are in the Outer Radius slot of a record with no Inner Radius. From v99 the extra word appears.
  - The code stores those as `inner_radius = 512` and `radius = 0` (or the IS radius, 10000 for `CarNukeTEST`).
- **Impact**:
  - 15 vanilla EXPL have a wrong `radius`, and `inner_radius` holds the outer value.
  - `systems/mines.rs` takes `blast.radius.max(blast.inner_radius)`, which happens to mask this for the 0-radius cases.
  - Only mine explosions are consumed today, and the vanilla mine explosions are modern forms, so there is no live symptom.
- **Suggested Fix**:
  - Infer the Inner Radius word from the record length: `DATA` ≥ 64 bytes means it is present, and 52 to 60 bytes means it is absent. The sizes are 52, 56 and 60 below v97, and 64 or more from v99.
  - Alternatively, thread the record form version into `parse_expl`. Add a fixture for a 52-byte record.

### ESM-2026-10-31-02: FO76 `EXPL` `DATA` is decoded at the Oblivion/FO3 offsets, and FO76 `PROJ` `DNAM` is not decoded
- **Severity**: LOW
- **Dimension**: Sub-Record Byte Accounting
- **Record / Sub-record**: `EXPL` / `DATA`, `PROJ` / `DNAM` (FO76)
- **Location**: `crates/plugin/src/esm/records/misc/effects.rs:447-475` (`parse_expl` falls through to the legacy `DATA` arm for `Fallout76`); `:236` (the PROJ `DNAM` arm is gated to `Fallout4`)
- **Status**: NEW (a gap in the per-game gating added by `c36ab4125`)
- **Description**:
  - FO76 `EXPL` `DATA` is 84 to 96 bytes, with a Damage Curve Table FormID at @24, Force @28, Damage @32, Inner @36, Outer @40.
  - Only `Skyrim | Fallout4` take the new arm. FO76 reaches the Oblivion/FO3 arm (`damage` @8, `radius` @12), which reads two FormIDs as floats.
  - FO76 `PROJ` carries the same 93-byte `DNAM` as FO4 (530 records, `DATA` empty). The `DNAM` arm is gated to `Fallout4`, so every FO76 projectile has zero flags, speed, range, alt-trigger and explosion.
- **Evidence**: `crExplosionGrenadePumpkin` (v210, 96 B) has `0xd8f99` at @0, `0x5303f7` at @4 and `0x0` at @8. The FO3 arm gives `damage = f32(0)` and `radius = f32(0x1f5b0)` ≈ 1.1e-40. The real values are Force 550, Damage 1, Outer 500 at @28/@32/@40.
- **Impact**: none today. The mine path keys on `is_proximity_mine` (FO76 projectiles are all zero) and no FO76 consumer reads the other fields. It is wrong data waiting for a consumer, and `ExplRecord` still advertises `damage` and `radius` for FO76.
- **Suggested Fix**:
  - Add `Fallout76` to the PROJ `DNAM` gate, since the layout is identical.
  - Give `EXPL` a FO76 arm: skip the extra FormID, then Force @28, Damage @32, Inner @36, Outer @40. Keep the FormID word version-gated (xEdit says version 150).
  - Alternatively, decline to decode (leave zeros) for games without a sourced layout, as `parse_bptd` did in #6465.
