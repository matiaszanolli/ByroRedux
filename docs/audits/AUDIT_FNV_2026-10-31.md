## Findings

### MEDIUM

#### FNV-2026-10-31-01: FO3/FNV `EXPL` `DATA` damage and radius are read 4 bytes late; the new M43 mine system runs on them for 174 placed FNV mines
- **Severity**: MEDIUM. Real-data evidence (raw bytes and decoded values) plus static reading of the consumer. Not reproduced in a running engine.
- **Dimension**: 2 (ESM slice) feeding 1/5 (cell spawn and gameplay). **Owner of the mechanism**: `/audit-esm` (decode) and `/audit-gameplay` (mines).
- **Location**: `crates/plugin/src/esm/records/misc/effects.rs:430-466` (`parse_expl`, the `b"DATA" if sub.data.len() >= 16` arm, `skip_or_eof(8)` then damage then radius); wrong-layout unit test `:717-733` (`parse_expl_picks_edid_full_damage_radius`); consumer `byroredux/src/systems/mines.rs:66-86` (`install_mine_book`), `:196-320`.
- **Status**: NEW. The decoder arm dates from `6d889d70d` (R2 migration, 2026-05-24). Its only consumer, the mine system, landed in `c36ab4125` (2026-10-30). ESM-2026-10-31-01/02 cover the FO4 and FO76 `EXPL` layouts only, and that audit states "neither has a consumer today", which the mine system now contradicts.
- **Description**: xEdit's FO3/FNV `EXPL` `DATA` is Force (0), Damage (4), Radius (8), Light FormID (12), Sound 1, Flags, IS Radius, … The decoder takes damage at offset 8 and radius at offset 12. It therefore stores the Radius as `damage` and the Light FormID, reinterpreted as an f32, as `radius`. The unit test encodes the same shifted layout, so it cannot catch this.
- **Evidence** (raw `DATA` bytes from `FalloutNV.esm`, 52 B each; decoded as `f32 f32 f32 f32 u32 …`):
  - `MineFragExplosion` (0x0007078B): force 250, **damage 100**, **radius 192**, then `0x00014BFF` (a LIGH). The engine stores `damage` 192 and `radius` 1.19098e-40, a denormal.
  - `MineNVPowderChargeExplosion` (0x00109A0F): force 100, damage 75, radius 192, same light. The engine stores damage 192.
  - `C4Explosion` (0x00130044): force 1000, damage 250, radius 512. The engine stores damage 512.
  - `install_mine_book` on `FalloutNV.esm` yields **7** proximity-mine `PROJ`s, of which 4 have placements: 0x000043FA ×131, 0x00109A0D ×33, 0x0014C65C ×7, 0x00059449 ×3, **174 REFRs** in all. Each is in `cells.statics` with a model (`weapons\1handminedrop\*.NIF`) and each has `prox=100 timer=3`. (A further mine with `prox=0.5`, 0x0010418E, has no placements.)
  - The `MineSpec` they get is `damage` 192 / 256 / 450 / 512 and `inner_radius = outer_radius = 1.19e-40`.
- **Impact**:
  - `blast_damage()` returns 0 for every actor, because only `distance <= 1.19e-40` takes damage. A player or NPC who comes within 100 units arms the mine. After the 3 s fuse the mine is hidden and ledger-disabled, with no damage and no knockback. Before M43, FNV mines were inert statics.
  - The authored values (100 damage out to 192, falloff per `blast_damage`) are not reached.
  - Once GAME-D4-2026-10-31-02 (replacing `HitEvent` insert) and -01 (re-arm after reload) are considered, FNV mines would also share those defects. This finding is the data-side prerequisite.
  - The `TMEE` Story Manager event is not raised on FNV, because no FNV node roots it.
- **Suggested Fix**: Read `DATA` at xEdit's FO3/FNV offsets (damage 4, radius 8), set `inner_radius = radius`, and rewrite the unit test with a real 52-byte FNV `DATA` image. Add a real-data pin on `MineFragExplosion` (damage 100, radius 192) to `.github/real-data-pins.txt`. Consider gating `mine_system` on a load order where the mine rules are known to apply.

### LOW

#### FNV-2026-10-31-02: #6471 leaves 15 unscripted looping statics frozen at spawn (Hoover Dam turbine shafts, grinding wheels, Helios laser), and 122 scripted ACTI placements now depend on the ObScript VM
- **Severity**: LOW (fidelity, uncertain against the legacy engine). Static evidence on real data.
- **Dimension**: 1 / 4. **Owner**: `/audit-legacy-compat` (spawn policy).
- **Location**: `crates/core/src/animation/named_sequences.rs:28-30` (`spawn_handle`), `byroredux/src/cell_loader/spawn.rs` (the `spawn_sequence` selection).
- **Status**: NEW side effect of the #6471 fix (`69af7947a`, correct for doors, containers and activators).
- **Description**: Spawn now plays `Idle`, else `SpecialIdle`, else nothing. Previously it played the first listed sequence. 290 of the 802 FNV object meshes with a `NiControllerManager` carry an idle. Of the remaining 512, 99 have at least one non-clamped sequence and **58 have a looping first sequence** that used to run and now stays in the bind pose.
- **Evidence** (`/mnt/data/tmp/fnvprobe`, `Fallout - Meshes.bsa` plus `Update.bsa`, `FalloutNV.esm`):
  - The 58 meshes are used by 42 ACTI bases (122 REFRs) that carry an object script mentioning `PlayGroup` (`Lucky38LightScript` 37 bases / 79 REFRs, `CasinoBaseLightScript` 1, `OnLoadPlayForwardSCRIPT` 3 bases / 37 REFRs, `NVGrindingWheelActivatorScript` 1 / 5). They depend on the VM running that script, which `m47-3-obscript.sh` gates on Lucky 38 (not run here).
  - **15 REFRs of unscripted STAT bases** lost their only motion. These are `NVHooverDamTurbineShaft` ×8 (`Forward` loop, 0.33 s), `NVGrindingWheelAll` ×3 (`Forward` loop 9.33 s), `HVWaterBlood` ×2, `HeliosLaserSTATIC` ×1 and `GomorrahLightsAlwaysOn` ×1. None has an embedded standalone clip.
- **Impact**: Static shaft, wheel and laser models where the vanilla game very probably animates them. Whether the original engine starts a sequence on a script-less STAT with no `Idle` is not established in this repo (no guessing), so the severity stays LOW.
- **Suggested Fix**: Establish the legacy rule (Gamebryo 2.3 `NiControllerManager` activation on a placed object). If a single looping non-activation sequence is auto-played, extend `spawn_handle`: a manager whose only sequences are non-clamped loops and which has no script plays the first. Pin it with the census above.

#### FNV-2026-10-31-04: four FNV live gates sit in `UNCHECKED_SMOKE_GATES`; `m41-ragdoll.sh` has no missing-data contract at all
- **Severity**: LOW (test-gap)
- **Dimension**: 6. **Owner**: `/audit-tooling` / audit infrastructure.
- **Location**: `scripts/check-playable-smoke-contracts.sh:328-332` (`UNCHECKED_SMOKE_GATES`: `m41-ragdoll`, `p5-door-transition`, `p5-f5-f9-quicksave`, `p5-soak`).
- **Status**: NEW. #6461 (`de764ad3d`) seeded the shrink-only list from the then-current unchecked set. The audit skill treats an FNV entry in it as a finding.
- **Description**:
  - `p5-door-transition.sh`, `p5-f5-f9-quicksave.sh` and `p5-soak.sh` are FNV reference-fixture gates that call `smoke_require_data` (exit 77 on missing data), so they satisfy SKIP != PASS, but the checker never executes that path for them. `p5-f5-f9-quicksave.sh` also has its own exit-77 branches for the Vulkan validation layer.
  - `m41-ragdoll.sh` (FNV Doc Mitchell, 18-body ragdoll) carries no `exit 77` or fixture guard at all, so a dataless runner would fail or mis-report instead of skipping.
- **Impact**: The FNV ragdoll and P5 hardening gates are the ones whose absence of data would be reported red or silently mismeasured.
- **Suggested Fix**: Move the three P5 gates into the fixture-parameterised loop (they already exit 77). Give `m41-ragdoll.sh` the fixture skip pattern, then remove all four from the list. The m43-* gates are Skyrim/FO4 and are not FNV findings; REG-2026-10-31-04 owns the rest of that list.

#### FNV-2026-10-31-05: sky planes, the fake sky dome and one lit waterfall sheet still synthesize solid trimesh colliders in FNV cells
- **Severity**: LOW. The predicate result is reproducible, but whether it makes a wall in play is not verified.
- **Dimension**: 1 (cell spawn), FNV data through the PHYSAL missing-collision fallback. **Owner**: `/audit-physics` / `/audit-nifal`.
- **Location**: `byroredux/src/cell_loader/spawn.rs:76-90` (`render_geometry_can_collide`), `:92-111`.
- **Status**: NEW. This is the residue after #6459, which keys on unlit plus alpha-blended.
- **Description**: A post-#6459 census (`/mnt/data/tmp/fnvprobe`, `bin/vfx.rs`) of STAT bases with no `bhk` whose meshes still pass the predicate gives, outside plain clutter and architecture:
  - **Sky backdrop meshes**: `WastelandSkyPlane` ×45, `FakeSkyDome01` ×11, `WastelandSkyPlaneOverheadFlat` ×8, `WastelandSkyPlaneOverheadDome` ×2, 66 REFRs in total. They are opaque `MATERIAL_KIND_NO_LIGHTING`, a 295×295 plane or a 543-radius half-dome, placed in about 30 interior cells (caves, sewers, shacks, mines). The rule deliberately keeps "unlit opaque" solid so that signs still collide.
  - `NVWaterfall` ×4: a 1,000-unit waterfall NIF whose opaque lit sub-sheet (`kind=0`, 204 vertices, about 240×620 units) passes while its blended unlit sheets are excluded.
  - `FXFlySwarm` ×31 (sub-metre geometry), `SecAnimFX` / `SecAnimFX2` and `ScorchedEarthEast/West`.
- **Impact**: Possible invisible walls at cave openings and sky holes, or a closed collision shell around a shack interior. Not verified in a running engine. The broader "STAT with no bhk gets an architecture trimesh" policy is by design; this is only the FNV VFX and backdrop tail of it.
- **Suggested Fix**: Add an explicit backdrop signal at the NIFAL boundary instead of per-mesh material guesses. One option is to key on the authored sky-plane / effects-directory identity at translation, or to exclude non-visible-bound `NO_LIGHTING` opaque meshes with no vertex alpha. Extend the #6459 real-data test with these bases.
