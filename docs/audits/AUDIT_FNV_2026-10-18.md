#### FNV-2026-10-18-01: `saved_type_shape_changes_require_format_major_bump` is red on `main`
- **Severity**: HIGH per `_audit-severity.md` ("Save: serialized-shape change without a `FORMAT_MAJOR` bump / baseline refresh"). Dimension 1 rated the same defect MEDIUM because it is not a save-compat break; I kept the table's rating and record the dispute here.
- **Origin**: D1-01 + D5-03 (merged). **Dimension**: save state around the cell round trip. **Owner**: `/audit-save`.
- **Location**: `byroredux/src/save_io/serde_default_guard_tests.rs:1112` / `:1230` (`BASELINE_SHAPE_FINGERPRINT = 0x244d_dcf0_0b26_0495`, `BASELINE_MAJOR = 111`); trigger `317320437` and `6f44874ac` (`byroredux/src/save_io/registry.rs`: `byroredux_scripting::challenges::register_saved`, `ChallengeState` at `crates/scripting/src/challenges.rs:340`, plus the obscript-ledger column).
- **Status**: NEW. Related but different: #5459 (bump ledger in `snapshot.rs` stops at v32).
- **Description**: The baseline fingerprint was last refreshed in `4b99e2393`. The two later commits registered new saved columns without touching the guard or `snapshot.rs`.
- **Evidence**: `cargo test --release -p byroredux --bin byroredux`: `saved serialized type shape changed without updating FORMAT_MAJOR/baseline; actual=0x6195e31318a87d92`.
- **Impact**: `main` is red, and the guard is blind to any later shape drift until someone refreshes the constant. Mitigating: `restore_resources` leaves a live resource untouched when its column is absent from an older snapshot (`crates/save/src/driver.rs` ~200), so an old save should load; real-save impact was not measured. FNV-visible because the challenge system is New Vegas-only.
- **Suggested Fix**: Decide bump-or-refresh per the guard file's ledger (an additive new resource may only need a deliberate fingerprint refresh, #4612), and make that change in the commit that adds the type.

### MEDIUM

#### FNV-2026-10-18-02: `AddScriptPackage` skips the Dialogue installer gate, so a declined Say-To / off-target package replaces an NPC's behaviour with nothing
- **Origin**: D5-01. **Dimension**: Ambient AI × M47.3 ObScript.
- **Location**: `byroredux/src/npc_spawn/ai_package.rs:1017-1031` (`scripted.or_else(select_active_package …)` then `AmbientBehavior::from_package`) and `:1079-1083` (`clear_ambient_behavior`, install only `if let Some(behavior)`); compare `dialogue_installs_ambient` at `:80`.
- **Status**: NEW. Introduced by `af5db229d`. Same shape as the fixed #5499 / #5722. `gh` search "AddScriptPackage" is empty.
- **Description**: #5722 made selection skip any Dialogue package the installer declines. The new script-package slot is read before that scan, so a declined package becomes `active` unconditionally. `from_package` returns `None`, the actor's previous behaviour is cleared, and nothing is installed. The slot stays and `mark_running` starts its clock. Must-complete packages (`PKDT` flags `0x3004`) never lapse, and a Dialogue package never stamps `Traveled`.
- **Evidence**: Probe over `FalloutNV.esm`: 729 `AddScriptPackage` call sites with a resolvable PACK, 171 of them Dialogue packages over 104 distinct packages. **27 of the 104 are statically declined** (5 Say-To, 22 targeting an NPC rather than the player), at **30 call sites in 7 scripts**; 26 of the 27 are Must-complete. Example: `ArcadeHooverDamTriggerScript` gives follower Arcade Gannon the Say-To package `ArcadeHooverDamDialoguePackage` and never removes it. The existing tests (`a_script_package_preempts_the_scheduled_package` and siblings) use only Travel packages. Not reproduced live.
- **Impact**: An FNV NPC (a companion in the Arcade case) stands idle, without its schedule or follow behaviour, until a script calls `EvaluatePackage` / `RemoveScriptPackage`.
- **Suggested Fix**: In the scripted branch, apply `dialogue_installs_ambient` to Dialogue-procedure packages and drop the slot (or fall through to the schedule) when the installer declines. The proper fix is a Say-To runtime, which also covers the five Say-To arcade packages #5722 declines. Add a Dialogue-type test beside the Travel ones.

#### FNV-2026-10-18-04: `m-exteriors.sh fnv boundary` hard-fails the live LOD-coverage gate with 384 overlapping terrain-LOD pairs
- **Origin**: D1-03. **Owner**: `/audit-exterior`. **Not a regression**: identical numbers on the baseline build `5d31fd637`; onset unknown.
- **Location**: producer `byroredux/src/streaming_helpers.rs::update_lod_coverage` (`state.lod_blocks`); selection `byroredux/src/cell_loader/lod_bands.rs`; residency `terrain_lod*.rs`.
- **Status**: NEW. #2371 and #4504 / #4505 are the construction-proof tickets, not this live result.
- **Description**: Each of the 24 coarse terrain-LOD blocks (L16 / L32) stays resident alongside the ~16 finer L4 / L8 blocks inside its footprint (12 × 32 = 384).
- **Evidence**: `lod-coverage: sampled=1 settled=1 overlaps=384 full_detail_overlaps=0 vwd_full_model_overlaps=0 terrain_churn=0 object_churn=0 terrain_resident=460 object_resident=285 verdict=FAIL`, on HEAD and on `5d31fd637`. Instrumenting a baseline-tree copy printed key histogram `{object L4: 285, terrain L4: 238, L8: 196, L16: 12, L32: 12}`, terrain overlaps 384, object overlaps 0, e.g. `(8,-64,24) vs (32,-64,0)`. The earlier "FNV: 0 overlaps" census claims are about the authored object-LOD index, not this engine-generated terrain ring. `settled=1` and `terrain_churn=0`, so it is a stable state, not a transient.
- **Impact**: two terrain-LOD surfaces cover the same far-field ground after a boundary traversal. User-visible effect (z-fight or double draw) is unproven; the gate that guards it is red for the reference title.
- **Suggested Fix**: find why the ring keeps both a coarse block and the finer blocks it supersedes after the three crossings; run the same gate on Skyrim to see whether it is FNV-specific.

#### FNV-2026-10-18-05: The default radius-12 door exit to WastelandNV saturates the 4 M-vertex geometry pool; 352 distant-LOD uploads are rejected
- **Origin**: D1-06. **Owner**: `/audit-exterior` / `/audit-renderer`.
- **Location**: `byroredux/src/scene.rs:40` (`DEFAULT_EXTERIOR_RADIUS = 12`; door transitions use it via `app_step.rs::exterior_transition_radius`); `crates/renderer/src/mesh.rs:30-35` (`VERTEX_POOL_HARD_CAP = 4_000_000`, strict admission).
- **Status**: NEW for FNV. `docs/engine/exterior-grid-streaming.md:133` documents the capacity behaviour only in general terms.
- **Evidence**: P0 route at the default radius: 625 full-detail cells, 106,749 entities, 3,759 meshes. Then `Object-LOD '…level4.x-8.y28.nif' mesh upload failed: vertex pool hard cap exceeded: would grow from 3984783 to 4001805 (cap 4000000)`, followed by **352** `Failed to upload LOD terrain block … scene geometry admission is closed after reaching the resident pool limit` and object-LOD equivalents, while `Exterior LOD rings settled around (-17,0)` is still logged. At `--radius 3` the same route logs 0 admission failures. One `SLOW FRAME dt=20241 ms` also occurred at radius 12 (plus 3.1 s and 1.6 s frames); the 20 s frame is not attributed and may have coincided with console calls on the 106k-entity world.
- **Impact**: after the default door exit the player gets full detail to 12 cells and no distant terrain or object LOD beyond it. Admission stays latched closed until unload compaction reopens it, and the reconcile reports "settled" anyway.
- **Suggested Fix**: reserve pool headroom for the LOD rings, or cap the full-detail radius by pool pressure; make "settled" fail when uploads are rejected.

## Observations (not findings)

- **`state_hash` drift.** Bench `state_hash` differs from the record at identical entity, light and TLAS counts, with 62 against 63 raster commands and a 0.8 BU shift of the bench camera in z. The hash covers draws, lights, water and bone matrices, so some content or placement changed since 10-13. Candidates: the ObScript-before-spawn ordering (`6b9397ed2`) or an NPC pose change. No frame-time effect; ROADMAP's R6a-stale-29 already asks for a same-machine control of `a3a7a43b0`, which was not done.
- **`GetIsCreature` / walk strides per gender and child.** Neither was measured live. Clip presence in the archive was confirmed, and the strides above come from a probe over the KF files.
- **INFO `DATA` Flags 2 (Say Once a Day)** (10-12 finding D2-01, #5747, closed). The fix was documentation only. `flags2` is still decoded and never consumed; 105 FalloutNV.esm INFOs and 89 DLC INFOs are affected.
- **Plugin guards red from `317320437`**: `no_module_redeclares_a_local_subrecord_builder` and `every_record_parser_takes_a_remap_or_is_explicitly_exempt`. Already tracked as open #6044; the parsed data is correct (105 CHAL, 20 MUSC).
