## What is now measured (vanilla `Fallout4.esm`, 2026-09-08)

- **Switchboard**: 174 absorbed REFRs, **141** non-STAT/SCOL — the figure the comment quotes, confirmed exactly. Breakdown: `MSTT 60, CONT 41, STAT 33, FURN 27, ACTI 10, TERM 3`.
- **232 interior cells** carry at least one non-STAT/SCOL absorbed REFR, **21 769** in total.
- Worst offenders: `InstituteConcourse` 571, `Vault75` 477, `GNN01` 429, `HalluciGen01` 411, `Vault111Cryo` 380, `MedTekResearch01` 378.

So the impact estimate in this issue is low by roughly two orders of magnitude. Switchboard is not the outlier it reads as — it is mid-table, and `MedTekResearch01` being on the list means a bench-of-record scene is affected either way the contract resolves.

## The proposed comparison was run, and is inconclusive

The suggested fix was to compare a Switchboard XPRI `FURN`/`MSTT` against the decoded CSG object set. The `.csg` itself carries no FormIDs — it is a geometry blob addressed by `(filename_hash, data_offset)` — so there is nothing to join on there. The `_oc.nif` side does carry identity: Switchboard's 60 bakes hold 1604 packed objects, and their nodes are named `CLONE <SourceMeshName>` (`CLONE SteamTStairsBlock01`, `CLONE IndCat1WaySide01`, …), 34 unique.

Matching those against the absorbed REFRs' base meshes gives **zero of the 141 non-STAT meshes** present in the bake — which looks like a clean answer for "the geometry is NOT baked, so retaining it is correct".

It isn't. The control fails: **zero of the 5 absorbed STAT meshes appear either**, and those are the ones the bake is known to contain. So the `CLONE` name set is drawn from a different population than the absorbed base meshes (34 names against 1604 objects suggests most baked nodes are unnamed), and the comparison is inconclusive in *both* directions rather than evidence of absence.

Reporting a negative result whose control fails would be worse than reporting nothing, so: not settled.

## What would settle it

Per-object identity that the `.csg`/`_oc.nif` pair does not expose, or a visual A/B of one cell — load Switchboard with the retain gate on and off and look for co-located duplicate furniture. The latter is cheap for anyone with the cell open and is the obvious next step; it is the kind of check `cargo test` structurally cannot make.

## What landed anyway

The issue's own requirement that "either way the two comments must stop contradicting each other" is done, across all three sites (there was a third: `cell_loader/precombined.rs` claimed the bake covers "architecture STAT placements", which agreed with neither of the other two):

- `precombine_can_replace_record` no longer claims dropping the REFR loses visuals. It states what is measured, states the open question as open, and says why the renderable stays for now.
- `esm/cell/walkers.rs` notes that "absorbed" is the ESM's word and not a promise that every listed REFR's geometry is baked.
- `cell_loader/precombined.rs` no longer implies the bake is STAT-only.

**The renderable is deliberately still spawned.** A duplicate mesh is a visible artefact someone will notice; suppressing one that is not actually baked silently deletes authored furniture from 232 cells, and `cargo test` sees neither outcome. That asymmetry is why this stays open rather than being fixed on the strength of an inconclusive measurement.
